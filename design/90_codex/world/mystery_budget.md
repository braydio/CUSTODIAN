# Mystery Budget

Status: candidate
Category: world
Priority: P0
Maturity: vibe
Cost: low
Owner: Brayden
Last reviewed: 2026-07-08

## One-line pitch

Reserve unanswered questions for places where a defined event and its consequences make uncertainty worth the player's attention. Most places may tell complete, understandable stories.

## Problem it solves

Overexplaining can destroy wonder, but compulsory ambiguity creates repetitive pseudo-mysteries and makes the world less credible.

## Why it fits CUSTODIAN

The world should feel ancient and partially understood because its surviving material history is rich, uneven, and consequential, not because every record or machine is inexplicably contradictory.

## Player-facing effect

Players constantly wonder:

Why is the ocean glowing?

Who built the Return Causeway?

Why are bells still ringing?

What is offshore?

## Systems touched

Lore, environmental storytelling, dialogue, quest design.

## Dependencies

None.

## Risks

Can become confusing, predictable, and emotionally thin if unanswered questions replace authored causes, concrete consequences, or eventual answers.

## Minimal version

For each candidate mystery, first record the underlying event, surviving physical proof, whose knowledge is limited, and the player's possible payoff. Omit mysteries when those answers are not defined. Do not enforce a mystery quota per biome.

## Full version

Plan mystery and revelation selectively during level design. Also plan ordinary knowable histories, practical human decisions, and unequivocal discoveries so the exceptional mysteries retain force.

## Graduation criteria

Candidate philosophy only; not a content quota or automatic generation requirement. Graduate only through the active design authority.
