# Operator Art Style Bible

## Status

V2 authoring authority. Structural rules are enforceable; artistic tolerances
remain advisory. The approved Operator 2.5D visual identity, accepted 128px
registration, canonical comparison reference, and no-cleanup disposition are
documented in `OPERATOR_2_5D_VISUAL_CONTRACT.md`.

## Projection

Operator presentation uses an elevated three-quarter camera language. Near and
far describe screen projection; they do not silently assert anatomical left or
right. Near limbs may occlude far limbs, the hood top plane remains readable,
and east/west cloak trails bias opposite travel and slightly screen-down. No
numeric camera angle is authoritative until calibrated against gameplay.

## Pixel contract

- Integer coordinates and opaque pixel clusters are the default.
- V2 never scales, rotates, filters, or resamples authored parts.
- Head scale, baseline, torso proportion, crossover silhouettes, grip, and
  weapon-tip continuity are review concerns across frames.
- Semantic edits draft first. A bake may expose gaps and must report that risk.
- Provisional metrics produce warnings, not fabricated artistic truth.

Workbench V2 remains the only canonical publication gate.

The backward-readable profile registry is stored in
`custodian/content/data/operator/authoring/operator_art_profile.json`. The
accepted structural 96x96 registration remains `legacy_96`; the accepted
128x128 body/reference profile does not change runtime selectors. Neutral
head/hip/knee guide coordinates are review references, not pose acceptance
criteria. `enforcement.artistic` remains false; action-specific recoil,
crouch, anticipation, and weight transfer remain authored motion.
