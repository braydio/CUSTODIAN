## Why this register exists

The Hall review found complementary ideas worth **weaving into future packet *refreshes***, rather than issuing 15 premature implementation workstreams. Each entry below has (1) an earliest existing planning/refresh seam, (2) prerequisites/evidence, (3) the **mechanics and implementation questions that must be decided when refreshed**, and (4) a concrete, falsifiable first slice. A future agent should neither skip these decisions nor treat a candidate card as already approved.

**User's selected player direction:** a **large, finite, seeded, procedurally generated Campaign World**, continuous on-foot/vehicle geography, persistent terrain/location identity, on-demand materialization, selectively authored lore locations, with room for later expansion. This is a **preferred design hypothesis/player-experience direction**, not an F15-B topology/coordinate/streaming lock; measured F15-A results and a human architecture decision are still required. Preserve recurring visits to the *same* Domain without rerolling its identity.

**Do not modify the currently authorized F15-A audit packet.** At this review `CAMPAIGN_GEOGRAPHY_RESIDENCY_AUTHORITY_AUDIT.md` and its fresh review pair have `ready/auto` headers on main (post-promotion). F15-A remains read-only and must complete independently. Its output is an input to the *next* design refresh, not permission to add unreviewed experiments to the claimed audit.

## Individual ideas: earliest hook, missing design, first proof

### A. Next F15 geography refresh: design early, implement after F15 locks

1. **Encounter Language** · [card](../../90_codex/world/encounter_language.md) · `candidate/P0/low-cost`. **Hook:** F15-B semantic generator/settlement-approach design, then F15-C proof. **Decide:** data-driven motif ID, visual cue→actual threat/reward contract, biome/structure variations, conflict with lore landmarks, repetition/false-positive budget and how authored locations opt in. **First proof after F15-B:** a *small canonical motif set* placed across two seeded environments, each cue grounded in actual generated encounter/loot/route state; no accidental misleading marker; legibility verified from Operator camera geometry before subjective visual sign-off. **Not:** an immediate new spritesheet or arbitrary decorations.
2. **Landmark Hierarchy** · [card](../../90_codex/world/landmark_hierarchy.md) · `candidate/P1/low-cost`. **Hook:** F15-B route/topology and worldgen planning. **Decide:** dominant / supporting / local landmark placement quotas scaled by real camera visibility and meaningful traversal, persistent IDs, skyline/approach silhouette and authored-site exceptions. **First proof:** two seeded runs show repeatable distant destination, intermediate navigation cues, stable reload and route recall; do not assume historic card's exact 1+3 ratios are a production performance lock.
3. **Spatial Compression** · [card](../../90_codex/world/spatial_compression.md) · `candidate/P1/low-cost`. **Hook:** F15-B geography/terrain; F15-C player-facing crossing. **Decide:** visibility horizon, vantage and occluder rules, far/near vista alignment, truthful distance versus travel time, stage/materialization seam; no non-Euclidean cheating. **First proof:** measurable sightline from departure through approach to destination with consistent world orientation and no pop-through; compare two seeds and actual foot travel times.
4. **Mystery Budget** · [card](../../90_codex/world/mystery_budget.md) · `candidate/P0/low-cost, vibe maturity`. **Hook:** F15-B region/storyteller semantic design, later World Autopsy. **Decide:** mystery *type*, site/faction/lore ownership, unanswered clue density, reveal boundaries and continuity; reserve authored canon facts, never procedurally contradict locked lore. **First proof:** region record tracks a small number of deliberate unanswered questions plus observable clues and explicitly *non-required* answers; no busywork collectible quota.
5. **Procedural Ruin Generator (schema only early)** · [card](../../90_codex/world/procedural_ruin_generator.md) · `candidate/P2/high-cost`. **Hook:** F15-B **only** reserve stable site/room purpose, failure-cause descriptor and clue/repair surfaces for later integration. The actual damage/prop/loot generator belongs to a **separate post-F15-B design lock** once accepted site geometry, material tags and narrative ownership are known. **Decide later:** cause→damage propagation/exit paths, affordance and nav safety, persistent repair aftermath, Asset V2 palette/props, reproducibility. **First playable proof:** 2–3 cause profiles across a few valid rooms, cohesive damage/clues with unchanged map navigation and actual source distinction. No asset request before visual grammar and content budget approval.

### B. Post-geography causal layer: decide in dependency order

6. **World Autopsy** · [card](../../90_codex/world/world_autopsy.md) · `candidate/P0/high-cost`. **Hook:** after F15 location IDs + WorldHistory/event provenance audit, not before. **Decide:** historical-cause generation vs observed runtime event authenticity; stable location-scoped event time (canonical fixed ticks for simulated events), authoritative writers, log confidence/incompleteness, bounded retention and read-only terminal query. **First proof:** one terminal reconstructs a short truthful event chain for a named site, ties to visible evidence and survives revisits; inferred backstory explicitly marked as inferred, never fabricated live history. **REMAP-3** still owns process-restart persistence.
7. **Line-of-Communication Graph** · [card](../../90_codex/world/line_of_communication_graph.md) · `candidate/P1/high-cost`. **Hook:** after F15 geographic route/backbone accepts real stable nodes/edges; define early shared *interface* when F14-C2 wants knowledge/reinforcement provenance. **Decide:** typed traversable route vs power/signal/logistics overlays, ownership of topology vs infrastructure consequences, physical edge evidence (road/bridge/cable/mast), incident/repair/hostility semantics, whether to extend `WorldStateGraph` without a competing owner. **First proof:** a *real* bridge/relay connectivity change affects one independently existing consumer and can be queried/inspected with identical seeded state; F15 owns geographic edges, not this subsystem.
8. **Faction Knowledge** · [card](../../90_codex/ai/faction_knowledge_system.md) · `candidate/P1/high-cost`. **Hook:** F14-C2 population/identity/residency contracts first, then shared signal/report path. **Decide:** source-tagged last-known reports, confirmation/rumor/decay, witness death or intercepted report, deterministic dissemination through approved graph; visible tells for player fairness. **First proof:** a scout must report across one working link; eliminate/isolate scout or break link and faction *does not* gain otherwise unknowable information.
9. **World Event Timeline** · [card](../../90_codex/world/world_event_timeline.md) · `candidate/P1/medium-cost`. **Hook:** F14-D/history and accepted Domain location identity after F15-B. **Decide:** one canonical `SimulationKernel` fixed-tick event schedule, durable event IDs, idempotent conditional/cancel behavior, recorded history, bounded forecasts; avoid new timer or world clock. **First proof:** one geographically scoped event fires once, cancel/replay/snapshot keep consistent event and consequences. Separate **ambient-only** effects from simulation-authoritative events.
10. **Resource Economy Graph** · [card](../../90_codex/world/resource_economy_graph.md) · `candidate/P1/high-cost`. **Hook:** after route/power/logistics provenance, canonical resource IDs and a concrete repair/fabrication consumer. **Decide:** supply/demand/consumption/shortage as *derived projections*, current strategic resource owner, typed logistics edges, budgeted offscreen updates, why a player cares; avoid a second economy ledger. **First proof:** a cut/restored route alters one real repair material bottleneck and terminal/Observatory explains why, with no double resource deductions.

### C. Later behavior, atmosphere, tools: do not strand, do not duplicate

11. **Sector Activity Simulator** · [card](../../90_codex/simulation/sector_activity_simulator.md) · `candidate/P1/high-cost`. **Hook:** F14-C2/F14-D review *of what already exists*. **Decide:** which offscreen consequences are truly not covered by accepted `AbstractActivitySimulationState`/kernel. **First proof:** one additional bounded meaningful site consequence on the existing clock, with exclusive ownership, identity and causal receipt; never stand up a second sector simulator by default.
12. **Ambient Scheduler** · [card](../../90_codex/world/ambient_scheduler.md) · `candidate/P2/medium-cost`. **Hook:** first approved multi-biome weather/ambient F15-C location. **Decide:** presentation-only timed variation versus consequential F14 World Event Timeline, geography/biome event table, seeded variation, pause/capture and scene restoration. **First proof:** one non-disruptive sound/fog/bell environmental event scoped to site/biome and resumed without duplicating simulation consequences.
13. **AI Morale & Cohesion** · [card](../../90_codex/ai/ai_morale_cohesion_system.md) · `candidate/P2/medium-cost`. **Hook:** after real grouped enemy squads, stable leadership/combat telemetry and F14/C2 population transitions. **Decide:** faction-specific thresholds, suppression/leader-loss reports, visible retreat/regroup tells, what crosses physical/abstract boundary. **First proof:** one group measurably changes behavior after one witnessed loss, with readable cues and no invisible punitive stat buff.
14. **Director Memory** · [card](../../90_codex/ai/director_memory.md) · `candidate/P1/medium-cost`. **Hook:** after Faction Knowledge source provenance and real encounter diversity. **Decide:** telemetry-only adaptation versus what a faction can actually learn; prevents invisible omniscience and bias across seeds. **First proof:** one countermeasure occurs only after a witness/report event and has visible cause; seeded alternate without report does not adapt.
15. **Developer Replay System** · [card](../../90_codex/tooling/developer_replay_system.md) · `candidate/P1/high-cost`. **Hook:** when real F15/F14 cases require timeline diagnosis beyond **existing Moment Forge + Observatory capture/export**. **Decide:** what trace data are missing, bounded event ring/snapshot cadence, identity/provenance, how to reuse existing run trace export and Replay tools. **First proof:** replay one scoped transition/actor event sequence from compact data, no full video/screenshot recording or competing capture frontend.
16. **Performance Budget Manager** · [card](../../90_codex/tooling/performance_budget_manager.md) · `candidate/P1/medium-cost`. **Hook:** after F15-A travel/load/perf measurement and first multi-locality F15 proof. **Decide:** *extend* Observatory counters, perf incidents and thresholds already present; meaningful per-system budget/profile/CI thresholds from measured data. **First proof:** a simulated over-budget local workload produces one actionable tagged warning without runtime overhead/regression; original card's numerical budgets are **examples, not approved thresholds**.

## Mandatory refresh mechanics for future Codex packet authors

At each matching gate, **before authoring any implementation packet**:

1. Follow the authoring chat URL; read this register, the relevant indexed idea card, **current master roadmap**, active design spec, `CURRENT_STATE.md`, existing queue/claimed workstreams and source.
2. Mark each tracked idea for that gate: `incorporate-now`, `extension-of-live-owner`, `separate-next-slice`, `defer-with-trigger`, or `superseded-by-existing-owner`. Write an explicit reason and what was verified.
3. **Decide mechanics** with concrete player-facing consequence, data/provenance ownership, fields/events/APIs, procedural determinism, a minimal falsifiable fixture, estimated performance/content/Asset V2 burden, and what *not* to implement. Resolve user-facing subjective/scale/lore choices with ChatGPT/user; avoid guesses.
4. Either update an existing active spec to incorporate the feature or write a **small separate active design spec under `design/02_features/` or `design/04_architecture/`** once authorized. Do not use a Codex card as runtime authority. Track source card graduation/extension in the Codex index/README and master roadmap.
5. Refresh the relevant **existing F15/F14 task packet or planned slot** with exact dependencies, acceptance, fixed-seed/negative tests and review, only after prior packet's independent review landed. Prevent duplicate dispatcher identities. All queue promotions require targeted authoring preflight, index update and remote publication.
6. Update this register's state/next-gate entry, even if the decision is `defer`. **Never silently drop an idea** because dependencies are late. Include the user-provided authoring chat URL in handoffs.

**Ownership rule:** F15 geography owns stable locations/physical topology; F02 procgen owns generated geometry/presentation; F14 owns abstract actor consequences and authoritative ticks; F04 owns physical spawning/AI; WorldHistory records history but never invents causes; `WorldStateGraph` derives keyed state, not an alternative simulation engine; REMAP-3 owns disk persistence; F09 owns vehicles; F03 terminals only present/query authorized data. No competing runtime authority is approved here.
