## 2. Fresh creative possibility, not confirmed history

**Some contact, organism, influence, or intelligent presence from one of those disturbing adjacent continuities could have crossed into the historical city's Lattice infrastructure and made use of a surviving recovery facility.**

Crucial distinctions:
- The canon does **not** identify the Ash-Bell sightings with the alternate-continuity fragments. Do not assert our Gate occupier is exactly an Ash-Bell figure, `NON-RECIPIENT`, a member of a known species, or the Severing's origin.
- We do **not** yet know the crossing's route (Gate/other city port/old return path/imported person or material), time (pre-Severing, during, later), form or agency. The west Gate stair is the **site of a later approach**, not proof that the painted Gate itself is a functional incoming aperture.
- “Enslaved humanity” is a *terrified contemporary interpretation*, not a proven factual description. The effective horror is organized human action without discernible coerced/voluntary distinction. Avoid shackles, overseer thrones or cliché mind-control imagery as primary proof.
- “Set up shop” suggests **appropriation of facilities**. It need not show a villain lounging in a nest or explain a cosmological motive.

## 3. Three-layer architecture/presentation proposal

**Layer A: The old ossuary.** An immense, vertical funerary descent beneath the historical city. Weathered Custodian recovery bays and numbered stone/recovery-niche walls share a material language. At a glance, the player reads “catacombs” and anticipates a forbidding optional hard region.

**Layer B: The functioning lower Crèche.** Going deeper, the supposedly abandoned sections are better maintained than the route above. Silent operating lamps; mechanical drums that advance; old patient reception, triage, identity and deployment infrastructure in regular operation despite no approved personnel. Recovery frames face inward along processional lines, not outward toward return-to-post.

**Layer C: Reappropriation for an unknown purpose.** Original designation/recovery pathways have been rerouted into **sorting, assembly, migration or dispatch** flows, rather than reclaiming a Custodian's body. Identical orderly staging bays, numbered stations with no obvious destination, rows of empty paired footprints and entirely plausible still-humanoid distant witnesses. Keep the mystery: are humans being carried, cared for, mobilized, processed or doing something that merely resembles these? The architecture offers evidence without declaring agency or intent.

**Distinctiveness:** This is NOT the outdoor Sepulcher Gardens, the separate Chapel of Late Service, the Undergate Register, a generic skeleton crypt, or an already-confirmed hostile species. The sense of “hard place” comes from descent, apparently impossible infrastructure scale, ominous maintenance/occupation and return-later sealing, not mechanics designed in this slice.

## 4. Three-stage visual revelation, no mechanics

1. **Literal Crèche spawn/nearby early room:** A small barred sealed glimpse into unnaturally deep galleries bearing *old* recovery materials and *new* orderly directional processing: one line of white status lamps far below where no one should be working. This is a **vista only**, not a shortcut or extra passage. The current player wake is Zone01 `(0,160)`; Gate is Zone07 and not literally beside spawn.
2. **West Gate stairs:** A striking monumental seal over a downward shaft, a contrasting strip of maintained metal visible through cracked stone, silent but operational lamps, and distant repeating bays. It invites the player to *return later* without a key hint, future boss, interaction prompt or premature entrance. East Gate stair remains an **unnamed overlook of the dead historical city**. North remains mandatory Custodian Approach.
3. **Mid-game optional return, future creative target only:** From the other side of the same threshold, the player sees the occupied facility's immense actual depth and realizes the “ossuary” and recovery installation are the same compromised infrastructure. New layout and hard region implementation are beyond this exploratory design.

## 5. Competing interpretations to preserve

- **Actual arrival:** An unidentified presence crossed from some other continuity and established lasting local occupancy. Favored creative hypothesis, not canon.
- **Imported procedure:** Perhaps personnel, equipment, or a copied operating pattern crossed first, and the institution itself started organizing people for an unknown purpose without visible controller. Equally compatible with existing forensic uncertainty.
- **False attribution:** The facility may be carrying out a brutal but originally human continuity/evacuation program which resembles Null Warrant's fragments. The player may infer an occupier before evidence can establish one.

Do **not** decide whether the hidden influence is benevolent, hostile, parasitic, protective, singular, distributed, or comprehensible in this document. A further late revelation could support a stronger claim if users explicitly choose it.
