## Canonical cosmology

The old Lattice civilization built a civilization-scale transit and
continuity-access framework across otherwise stable reality. A reciprocal
propagation hazard moved through that framework. The Severing amputated the
network, but the damage had already destabilized regions connected to it.

Before the Severing, planets, settlements, ecosystems, machinery, and ordinary
physical processes existed independently of Archive Engines. The Lattice was
infrastructure imposed on stable reality; it did not create reality.

In the post-Severing regions relevant to CUSTODIAN, stable complex reality can
no longer persist indefinitely outside sufficient Lattice/Archive coherence
influence. A **Lattice Domain** is an existing, contiguous region of damaged
post-Severing reality maintained at usable physical coherence by one or more
Archive Engines and associated Lattice infrastructure.

Archive Engines stabilize coherence, arbitrate routes, and use telemetry,
calibration, history, and provenance as diagnostic reference. Records assist
stabilization; they never metaphysically determine what is real. Provenance is
forensic evidence lineage and continuity-origin verification.

The Pale is a real unstable continuity condition surrounding or intruding upon
Domains. It is not radiation, metaphor, forgotten records, or a source of
reality. Domain core, stable field, fringe, unstable coherence, and deep Pale
are a gradient rather than a binary map edge.

Surviving apertures, gateways, continuity ports, relays, and route systems may
connect Domains. Operational distance is route distance. Restoring a route is
both infrastructure repair and an exposure decision because the Reciprocal
Continuity Law remains absolute: any reachable continuity may potentially
establish a return path.

Most encountered Domains are survivable fragments of the damaged post-Severing
world, not automatically alternate universes. Continuity-displaced, adjacent,
historical, imported, or mixed destinations remain consequential anomalies.

## Contracts and runtime lifecycle

A Contract is a bounded operational intervention into a known, newly reachable,
partially characterized, or strategically relevant Lattice Domain. It does not
create a world. A `CampaignRegion` is a transient runtime representation of a
portion of a Domain; unloading or freeing that runtime object does not destroy
the represented place in fiction.

Domains persist after extraction unless their Archive field collapses, the Pale
consumes them, catastrophic continuity failure destroys coherence, or an
explicit story/system event changes their state. Canon permits later
revisitation when route, Hub, field, and strategy permit it; implementation may
defer free travel and inactive-domain simulation.

Archive field reinforcement expands coherent access to territory that already
exists but was previously unstable or inaccessible. It does not create terrain
ex nihilo. Fields may also contract, returning territory to fringe, instability,
or Pale.

## Preserved mysteries and history

The Reciprocal Continuity Law, the Severing, Pale uncertainty, provenance's
non-metaphysical role, the complete Ash-Bell sequence, Null Warrant uncertainty,
and the meaning of `NON-RECIPIENT` remain intact. Ash-Bell is a historical
case study of reciprocal coupling, not the universal cause of the cosmology.

Sundern Keep occupies part of a persistent Domain maintained by surviving
continuity-port and Archive infrastructure. Its imported architecture and
continuity anomalies remain exceptional consequences of damaged transit
infrastructure.

architecture is locked. New documents must not introduce dependencies on the
superseded roster as if it were newly finalized canon.

`Aperture`, `Continuity Port`, and `CampaignRegion` consistently. “Transient”
may describe a runtime object only. Rewrite ontological claims that Campaign

mission resolves. Runtime scene unloading remains valid and requires no code
change in this migration.
