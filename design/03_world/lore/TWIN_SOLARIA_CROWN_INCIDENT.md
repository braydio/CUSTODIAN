# Twin Solaria — The Crown Reciprocal Incident

> **Status:** canonical / active  
> **Scope:** Twin Solaria historical function, the missing Second Crown, the Crown
> Reciprocal Incident, the deliberate amputation, and its relationship to the
> civilization-scale Severing.  
> **Level authority:** `design/05_levels/TWIN_SOLARIA.md`  
> **Setting authority:** `design/03_world/lore/CORE_LORE.md`,

## 1. Canonical lock

Twin Solaria was built around **two enormous Crown-class aperture courts**.

Those two apertures are the namesake **Twin Solaria**.

They are not literal suns.

They are not the paired blue crystal towers.

The surviving facility contains:

- **Solarium I — Acquisition Aperture**, still physically present in the upper-left
  circular court;
- **Solarium II — Passage Aperture**, formerly occupying the now-missing upper-right
  circular court;
- the surviving root structure and cut interface of Solarium II, now commonly called
  **the Amputation**.

The current visual reference preserves the shape of this loss: the giant blank
upper-right region is not canonically an unfinished section of the facility. It is the
space where the Second Crown used to be.

---

## 2. The two Solaria had different jobs

The pair were not redundant gates.

They separated **knowing a route** from **committing to a route**.

### 2.1 Solarium I — Acquisition Aperture

Solarium I existed to acquire, interrogate, and observe a candidate route without
immediately authorizing full passage.

It could:

- resolve a remote destination strongly enough to inspect it;
- compare live destination behavior to Archive history;
- measure route coherence;
- test outbound correlation;
- expose a **Resolved Route Vista**;
- support reciprocity analysis;
- allow a route to remain observable while still held.

The golden landscape visible in the surviving reference image is a Resolved Route
Vista.

It is not local sunrise.

It is not ordinary sky.

A route visible through Solarium I is not automatically traversable.

### 2.2 Solarium II — Passage Aperture

Solarium II accepted an already reviewed route solution and created the controlled
passage state used for actual transfer of people, equipment, and material.

Full passage required:

1. Home Index convergence;
2. historical and live witness correlation;
3. outbound acquisition through Solarium I;
4. reciprocity review;
5. applicable Custodian / Null Warrant authority conditions;
6. handoff of the approved route solution to Solarium II.

This separation was itself a safety doctrine.

The civilization deliberately did not make "we can see it" equivalent to "we can
walk through it."

## 3. Supporting instruments

The tall blue structures near the upper court are continuity anchors, not the
Solaria themselves.

Use:

- **Outbound Anchor** — stabilizes the destination-side solution during acquisition;
- **Reciprocal Anchor** — stabilizes and measures the return-path solution;
- **Meridian Needle** — long-baseline route correlator;
- **Reciprocity Dial** — adjudication instrument presenting outbound and return-path
  evidence;
- **West Echo Drum** — controlled route interrogation;
- **East Echo Drum** — returned-response analysis;
- **Home Index** — local origin authority;
- **Archive Witness Ring** — historical evidence;
- **Live Witness Ring** — present-tense field evidence;
- **Coherence Witnesses** — distributed fail-closed consensus instrumentation.

This naming distinction is canonical.

## 4. The Crown Reciprocal Incident

**Crown Reciprocal Incident** is the surviving institutional name for the catastrophic
Solarium II event associated with the opening phase of the wider Severing crisis.

The event is not canonically established as the first event of the Severing.

It is one of the best-preserved high-order forensic sites from that crisis.

The important lock is:

> **Solarium II was already exhibiting impossible or unacceptable reciprocal behavior
> before the facility executed the physical amputation that destroyed it.**

Therefore two categories of damage coexist at the surviving root:

1. **pre-amputation anomalous damage**, which normal scuttle geometry does not fully
   explain; and
2. **deliberate emergency-separation damage**, produced when the court severed
   Solarium II from the surviving Crown Annex.

The event was both a failure **and** an intentional containment act.

## 5. What the surviving evidence proves

The following are canonical facts available to sufficiently deep investigation.

### 5.1 Solarium II was active

The Passage Aperture was not a centuries-dead ruin when the incident occurred.

Its transfer systems, anchors, Witness network, and isolation hardware were in
serviceable or actively operating condition.

### 5.2 A reciprocal condition exceeded the normal containment envelope

The exact initiating stimulus remains unresolved.

Surviving records support symptoms such as:

- return-path convergence failure;
- reciprocal address instability;
- unexpected inbound route behavior;
- Witness disagreement;
- route-state behavior that persisted beyond expected shutdown conditions.

Not every surviving record agrees on the exact symptom ordering.

### 5.3 The problem preceded the final physical cut

Forensic evidence at the surviving Solarium II root may establish that some damage,
field stress, or contradictory instrumentation state existed before the sacrificial
separation hardware fired.

Examples appropriate for later environmental evidence include:

- structural deformation outside the expected direction of the scuttle loads;
- protective glass fused on the wrong side of a housing;
- Witness records with mutually incompatible timestamps;
- a returned echo timestamped before the local interrogation pulse that should have
  generated it;
- loading on the far arc before the release bolts fired;
- route-state persistence after local aperture shutdown was requested.

These are evidence categories, not a requirement that every example appear in one
scene.

### 5.4 A deliberate Crown Amputation occurred

The surviving root also contains unmistakable designed separation evidence.

Appropriate evidence includes:

- sacrificial structural releases;
- guillotined continuity buses;
- isolation shutters;
- severed field trunks;
- controlled breakpoints;
- emergency authority interlocks;
- scuttle-state records.

The Amputation was not simply the result of an explosion.

Someone or something executed a containment procedure that the facility had been
designed to support.

### 5.5 Null Warrant containment authority was already present

Null Warrant did not appear only after the damage.

Surviving records establish that Null Warrant containment authority, route-interdict
capability, or classified instrumentation was already integrated into the Solarium II
safety chain.

This proves the old civilization anticipated Crown-class reciprocal failure as a
category.

It does **not** prove Null Warrant knew the ultimate nature of the hazard.

### 5.6 The final authorization chain is unresolved

Canon does not yet establish which authority issued the decisive physical-amputation
order.

Possible surviving participants include:

- Null Warrant;
- a Custodian;
- Meridian / civic route operations;
- an automatic fail-closed system;
- a layered concurrence among several authorities.

Do not casually resolve this in incidental dialogue.

The identity of the final authorizing authority is campaign-spine material.

## 6. The Severing is larger than Twin Solaria

Twin Solaria is not the whole Severing.

The Severing is the civilization-scale fragmentation, destruction, or deliberate
amputation of the wider Lattice network during a propagation crisis.

The Crown Reciprocal Incident is one local high-order event inside that larger
catastrophe.

The critical forensic fact is:

> **Local termination of Solarium II did not terminate the wider crisis.**

Surviving Crown records may show the Passage Aperture physically disconnected and its
local route state collapsing while unrelated route activity, emergency closures, or
network amputations continued elsewhere.

This is the moment the player can understand why destroying one gate was not enough.

The problem had already become network-scale, or was already occurring independently
at multiple network sites.

---

## 7. Ground Zero is deliberately unresolved

Do not establish Twin Solaria as the origin of the propagation hazard.

Twin Solaria may have been:

- an origin point;
- an early victim;
- a high-order amplifier;
- the first Crown-class facility to diagnose the pattern correctly;
- one of several simultaneous failures;
- a containment site responding to an event already underway elsewhere.

The phrase **FIRST CONFIRMED CROWN-CLASS RECIPROCAL FAILURE** is acceptable.

The phrase **FIRST RECIPROCAL FAILURE** is not.

"First confirmed" describes surviving institutional evidence.

It does not establish cosmic chronology.

## 8. Relationship to Null Warrant

Twin Solaria strengthens Null Warrant lore by proving the Office's fear predates the
final catastrophe.

The Crown court contains evidence that someone had already planned for:

- reciprocal contamination;
- route quarantine;
- high-order aperture isolation;
- destructive network surgery;
- refusal to complete a technically viable handshake.

This should create the question:

> **What had they seen before Solarium II?**

It should not immediately answer it.

### 8.1 Acceptable Null Warrant machine language

```text
RETURN-PATH CONTACT: UNRESOLVED
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: MULTIPLE
NULL WARRANT INTERDICT: ACTIVE
DO NOT COMPLETE PASSAGE HANDSHAKE
```

Later, more deeply sealed evidence may contain:

```text
CROWN RECIPROCAL INCIDENT
SOLARIUM II

PASSAGE AUTHORITY: REVOKED
RECIPROCAL CONVERGENCE: FAILED
NULL WARRANT INTERDICT: ACCEPTED

CROWN AMPUTATION:
AUTHORIZED
```

The second readout establishes that an amputation was authorized.

It does not establish who authorized it unless later campaign canon explicitly does
so.

## 9. Relationship to Ash-Bell

The Crown Reciprocal Incident and the Ash-Bell Unarrival may rhyme.

They are not presently identified as the same event or the same phenomenon.

Both may involve:

- reciprocal instability;
- unresolved route contact;
- Null Warrant fear;
- emergency containment;
- evidence that does not fit ordinary transit behavior.

But canon must preserve these open questions:

- Were the Ash-Bell figures related to the Crown Reciprocal Incident?
- Was either related to the original propagation hazard?
- Did Null Warrant apply one classification to multiple different phenomena?
- Did similar presentation cause authorities to conflate distinct hazards?
- Was one a symptom of a larger process rather than the process itself?

Do not use Twin Solaria to collapse all setting mysteries into one monster.

## 10. Relationship to NON-RECIPIENT

`NON-RECIPIENT` remains unresolved.

Twin Solaria may contain a sealed Null Warrant record using that designation, but such
a record is still an institutional hypothesis or classification.

It does not prove that:

- NON-RECIPIENT is an entity;
- NON-RECIPIENT caused the Crown Incident;
- NON-RECIPIENT caused the Severing;
- NON-RECIPIENT is the Ash-Bell humanoid presentation;
- NON-RECIPIENT traversed Solarium II.

The strongest acceptable form is:

```text
RETURN-PATH CONTACT: NON-RECIPIENT
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: UNRESOLVED
PASSAGE HANDSHAKE: PROHIBITED
```

The player may interpret that as terrifying.

The canon does not explain it for them.

## 11. The Amputation as a physical landmark

The missing upper-right portion of the current Twin Solaria composition is canonical.

It is not to be repaired in documentation art simply for symmetry.

The surviving east-side root should eventually read as industrial emergency surgery
performed on architecture.

The physical language should combine:

- clean designed separation points;
- catastrophic deformation that does not match those points;
- dead continuation buses;
- cut anchor conduits;
- surviving warning hardware;
- limited debris or structure disappearing toward the Crown Verge.

Do not add:

- giant claw marks;
- a monster-shaped impact;
- a conventional bomb crater;
- a readable alien projectile impact.

The site should support forensic interpretation rather than dictate one.

## 12. The Second Crown as campaign revelation

Player-facing understanding should escalate.

### Stage A — spatial suspicion

The player sees one enormous circular aperture and the apparently unfinished or
missing northeast mass.

The name "Twin Solaria" is not explained.

### Stage B — architectural recognition

A recovered plan, route diagram, or structural overlay proves there were two
apertures.

The current black region is absence, not design asymmetry.

### Stage C — functional distinction

The player learns:

- Solarium I acquired and inspected routes;
- Solarium II carried passage.

The surviving machine can find roads but cannot perform full Crown-class transfer.

### Stage D — the Amputation

Forensics establish that Solarium II was deliberately separated.

The old civilization had designed the court to destroy one of its own apertures.

### Stage E — pre-amputation anomaly

Evidence establishes that something was wrong before the scuttle fired.

The Amputation was a response, not the whole incident.

### Stage F — network-scale revelation

A final local log proves the wider crisis persisted after Solarium II was cut.

Twin Solaria did not contain the event by destroying its gate.

The Severing was larger.

### Stage G — restoration dilemma

The player eventually acquires enough knowledge to contemplate rebuilding or replacing
Passage capability.

The question is not:

> Can we repair the fast-travel machine?

It is:

> The last civilization deliberately cut this machine out of its own capital.  
> We now know enough to put it back.  
> What evidence would justify doing that?

## 15. Design intent

Twin Solaria should not explain the Severing.

It should make the scale of the Severing finally comprehensible.

The player sees a facility capable of reconstructing lost roads.

They discover that its missing half was intentionally torn away.

They discover that the tearing-away was not enough.

Only then does "The Severing" stop sounding like the name of an ancient disaster and
start sounding like what it actually was:

**civilization cutting its own roads faster than something could use them.**
