## 1. The Reciprocal Continuity Law

Any reachable continuity may potentially establish a return path.

This is the foundational physical law of CUSTODIAN's setting. The old civilization's Lattice network did not merely open doors outward. Every continuity reached represented not only a destination, but a potential route back. The reciprocal nature of continuity access was the hazard that ultimately led to the Severing.

---

## 2. The Severing

The Severing is the civilization-scale collapse, fragmentation, or deliberate amputation of the Lattice network.

The initiating cause remains unresolved. The leading hypothesis is that a continuity propagation hazard — something propagating through the return paths that had been opened — forced or motivated the destruction of the network. Whether this was disaster, attack, containment, or all three remains unknown.

The Severing destroyed or deliberately severed most Lattice routes. Continuities became isolated. Pale boundaries spread at damaged and unstable continuity interfaces. Old institutions shattered. The player does not initially know what crossed, whether anything intelligent crossed at all, or whether the Severing was catastrophe, defense, or sabotage.

---

## 3. The Pale

The Pale is a real cosmological phenomenon that manifests at damaged or unstable continuity boundaries.

It is not caused by provenance failure. It is not a metaphor for forgetting. It is a physical condition of reality at the edges where continuity has been damaged, severed, or left unstable.

The Pale remains unresolved. Its exact nature, relationship to the original continuity hazard, and whether it is related to or distinct from the propagation that caused the Severing are all open questions.

---

## 4. Lattice Technology

The Lattice is real continuity-field technology.

A Lattice creates a temporary reality pocket: a bounded field coherent enough to inhabit, resistant to Pale intrusion for a finite time, and dependent on Archive infrastructure for reinforcement.

Key properties:

- Temporary reality pocket
- Field coherence
- Pale resistance
- Finite lifetime
- Different time and universe possibilities

Lattices may couple compatible continuity states. A Lattice aperture is therefore both transport infrastructure and a possible reciprocal continuity path.

---

## 5. Provenance

Provenance is evidence lineage and continuity-origin verification.

It is not a metaphysical substrate. It does not make things real. Restoring provenance does not repair causality.

Provenance is the forensic discipline of determining where something came from, through what route it arrived, and whether its evidence chain is intact. This is an extremely important practical question — "Did this reactor come from our continuity?" — but it is an engineering and investigative question, not a metaphysical one.

Approved usage:

```text
PROVENANCE: UNKNOWN
PROVENANCE: ASH-BELL CONTINUITY
PROVENANCE CONFLICT: LOCAL MANUFACTURE RECORD ABSENT
SOURCE INTEGRITY: LOW
CONTINUITY ORIGIN: UNRESOLVED
```

## 6. Ash-Bell Protocol

The Ash-Bell Protocol is a real old-world emergency synchronization and continuity-containment procedure.

Authority: Meridian Office.
Stations: I through IX.
Required response: Answer.
Station IX officer: Precentor Orra.

The Protocol was designed to achieve regional continuity closure during a hazard event. Nine stations were required to synchronize. Each station issued an Answer confirming readiness. When all Answers arrived, regional closure could proceed.

---

## 7. The Ash-Bell Unarrival

The Ash-Bell Unarrival is the historical Station IX catastrophe.

Sequence:

1. Continuity hazard detected.
2. Ash-Bell Protocol initiated.
3. Nine Meridian stations ordered to synchronize regional closure.
4. A civilian evacuation corridor failed.
5. Orra received orders to proceed directly to Station IX.
6. Orra deliberately diverted to recover stranded civilians.
7. Stations I-VIII answered.
8. Station IX failed to answer within its required window.
9. System classified Station IX: UNARRIVAL.
10. Regional continuity closure completed incompletely.
11. The Open Interval began.
12. Ash-Bell continuity remained coupled to the local state.
13. People, matter, memories, structures, and/or state information crossed.
14. Orra eventually reached Station IX.
15. Orra gave the Ninth Answer.
16. The coupling collapsed.
17. Local catastrophe had already occurred.
18. Wider propagation was arrested.

---

## 8. The Open Interval

The Open Interval is the period between Station IX's missed response and the eventual Ninth Answer.

During the Open Interval, the Ash-Bell continuity remained physically coupled to the active world. Cross-continuity drift became possible. People, objects, memories, structures, and state information crossed the boundary.

The Ninth Silence is the folk and devotional name for the Open Interval.

---

## 9. Unarrival

Unarrival (single-n) originates as an Ash-Bell operational term.

It names the expected traversal, response, or synchronization failing inside its required interval. At Station IX, the system classified Orra's missed response as UNARRIVAL.

The term was later generalized by religious traditions, particularly the Pale Bell Penitents, who elevated a specific operational failure into universal theology.

The canonical spelling is **Unarrival** (single n). The older double-n spelling "Unnarrival" is retired.

---

## 10. Ash-Bell Continuity

The Ash-Bell Continuity is a real adjacent continuity that became strongly coupled to the active world during the Ash-Bell Unarrival.

Residual Lattice relationships continue to pull people, objects, memories, and physical states across that boundary. Cross-continuity drifters are real. The Ash-Bell Continuity is not a metaphor, damaged memory, or possible future.

---

## 11. Saint Orra

Canonical titles:

```text
Precentor Orra
Meridian Office
Station IX
```

Use **Precentor**, not Preceptor.

Later titles:

```text
Saint Orra
The Unarrived Saint
Saint of the Ninth
The Witness Who Came Late
```

Core phrase: **ORRA COMES LATE**

Possible meanings: accusation, warning, prayer, promise, reassurance.
Underlying emotional meaning: Late is not the same as never.

Orra's moral ambiguity is mandatory. All of these must remain defensible:

- She abandoned her post.
- She saved civilians.
- She enabled the Open Interval.
- She ended the Open Interval.
- She prevented wider catastrophe.
- Someone higher up created the impossible choice.

That is the wound.

---

## 12. Null Warrant Office

The Null Warrant Office is a real old-world continuity-hazard, secrecy, and containment authority.

Null Warrant handled:

- Continuity hazards
- Dangerous Lattice research
- Route quarantine
- Reverse traversal evidence
- Continuity contamination
- Network amputations
- Classified containment

---

## 13. NON-RECIPIENT

NON-RECIPIENT is an optional classified Null Warrant designation.

It is not a confirmed entity. The player can spend half the game wondering whether NON-RECIPIENT is a thing. Canon does not say yet.

Possible machine text:

```text
RETURN-PATH CONTACT: NON-RECIPIENT
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: UNRESOLVED
DO NOT COMPLETE HANDSHAKE
```
