# Legacy Interdiction Mesh

> **Classification:** `HAZARD_LAYER` — never a polity or eighth faction

## Identity

The Legacy Interdiction Mesh is the composable ecology of surviving automated
security, route denial, targeting, credential enforcement, zoning, quarantine,
and military command systems left by dead authorities. It has no beliefs,
citizens, diplomacy, culture, or unified command.

## Material Basis and Organization

Its components include turrets, patrol constructs, access gates, mine networks,
denial beacons, old drones, command relays, and linked checkpoints. Local pieces
may coordinate when obsolete credentials or network fragments align; this is
protocol interoperability, not political organization.

## What the Model Gets Right

Automated violence persists after its rationale, and the same physical hazard
can overlay any polity, tradition, ruin, or unoccupied route without implying a
new human constituency.

## Failure Mode

The Mesh is failure mode embodied: correct execution under dead context. It can
protect an empty warehouse, enforce a vanished border through a living town, or
deny evacuation because an all-clear will never arrive.

## Domain Policy

None. Individual systems execute inherited rules. Their combined effect may
partition territory, consume power, block routes, or preserve sites by accident.

## Custodian Relationship

Some nodes recognize Custodian credentials, some recognize conflicting higher
authorities, and some treat reappearing Custodian authority as the exact trigger
their interdiction order anticipated. Authentication is not universal control.

## Environmental Grammar

Overlapping warning eras, dead-government insignia, repeated credential gates,
patrol wear, fields of fire, no-entry corridors, still-powered control cabinets,
and modern settlements grown around lethal procedural gaps.

## Pre-Combat Behavior

Scan, challenge, classify, warn, escort, deny, track, and fire according to local
protocol. Systems do not posture, worship, negotiate values, or become angry.

## Gameplay Opportunities

Compose old rules into spatial puzzles; authenticate one layer while triggering
another; redirect a denial system; cross a route that modern residents understand
better than its machine; choose whether disabling protection exposes a settlement.

## Variation

Civil security, military interdiction, quarantine enforcement, industrial
safety, transit control, and corrupted mixed-command clusters should reuse
physical archetypes while producing different rules.

## Relations

Every polity adapts, exploits, repairs, fears, or contests local Mesh elements.
Those relationships are local facts and never convert the Mesh into a polity.
