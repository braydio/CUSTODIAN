# Cordon Service

> **Classification:** Major polity family

## Identity and Material Basis

The Cordon Service is a family of quarantine administrations, route-verification
corps, border clinics, contamination investigators, and emergency isolation
forces. It exists because an uncertain object, person, or path may carry a
reciprocal continuity risk that ordinary inspection cannot dismiss.

## Organization and Doctrine

Services may answer to city compacts, inter-Domain treaties, or autonomous
emergency mandates. Their doctrine is evidence before passage and bounded
uncertainty before irreversible contact.

## What It Gets Right

Provenance is an engineering and public-safety problem. Some compassionate
breaches really can spread catastrophe beyond the people immediately visible.

## Failure Mode

Emergency authority becomes permanent; suspicion becomes caste; isolation
substitutes for investigation; and preserving a clean chain matters more than
the people trapped outside it.

## Domain Policy

Verify routes, isolate uncertain arrivals, retain evidence, establish layered
cordons, and reopen through explicit thresholds rather than political pressure.

## Custodian Relationship

The Custodian is the best available continuity operator and a person with an
unreliable personal provenance. Services need that authority and may still
refuse to admit its bearer.

## Environmental Grammar

Nested perimeters, origin seals, clean/uncertain lanes, specimen cabinets,
route histories, decontamination stations, observation galleries, and doors
whose warnings explain what evidence is missing.

## Pre-Combat and Noncombat Behavior

Personnel interview arrivals, exchange sealed samples, maintain distance,
observe anomalies, and issue staged warnings. Hostility follows boundary
violation or failed verification, not automatic faction alignment.

## Gameplay Opportunities

People trapped across an uncertain gate; a route whose evidence chain was
forged; a quarantine whose test cannot finish before field failure; a choice
between rescue and propagation risk.

## Internal Variation

Clinical services, treaty cordons, militarized directorates, mobile route teams,
and radical provenance traditions—including Choir lineages—disagree on mercy
and acceptable uncertainty.

## Relations

Witness recognition contests machine proof; Orraic rescue contests delay;
Fieldworks pushes reopening; Charter argues jurisdiction; Recovery seeks access
to sealed value; Drawdown may abandon the quarantined zone entirely.
