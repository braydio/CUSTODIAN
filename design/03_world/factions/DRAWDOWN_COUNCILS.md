# Drawdown Councils

> **Classification:** Major polity family

## Identity and Material Basis

Drawdown Councils govern contraction. They emerged where finite field support
made retreat unavoidable and somebody had to choose what remained powered,
evacuated, archived, dismantled, or left beyond the maintained boundary.

## Organization and Doctrine

Some councils are elected district assemblies; others are emergency boards,
population ministries, or rotating technical juries. Their doctrine is that an
honest surrender can preserve more life than an unplanned collapse.

## What It Gets Right

Capacity is finite. Evacuation, decommissioning, and defensible borders require
planning before panic makes every choice worse.

## Failure Mode

Triage becomes destiny. Councils may condemn politically weak districts, hide
alternatives, normalize permanent emergency, or value clean numbers over lives
whose worth cannot be entered in a support ledger.

## Domain Policy

Reduce load early, retire infrastructure in order, preserve evacuation routes,
and concentrate population around maintainable services.

## Custodian Relationship

A Custodian may prove a condemned anchor repairable or reopen a retired route.
That can save a district—or destroy the legitimacy of every sacrifice already
made under the council's plan.

## Environmental Grammar

Numbered evacuation sectors, decommission marks, portable archives, dismantled
utilities, preserved corridors through empty blocks, ration boards, and civic
memorials that record what was deliberately surrendered.

## Pre-Combat and Noncombat Behavior

Wardens count people, inspect departure queues, isolate retired services, guide
evacuees, and prevent unauthorized reoccupation. Violence protects the plan,
not territorial pride.

## Gameplay Opportunities

Select a district for retirement; reopen a condemned facility; escort a staged
evacuation; decide whether population, industry, archives, or defensibility gets
the remaining support.

## Internal Variation

Participatory councils, austere actuarial boards, temporary evacuation cabinets,
and entrenched austerity regimes all exist.

## Relations

Fieldworks challenges contraction; Recovery profits from it; Witness Assemblies
contest who may be displaced; Orraic Orders demand late rescues; Cordon can make
temporary exclusion permanent; Charter Authorities formalize sacrifice.
