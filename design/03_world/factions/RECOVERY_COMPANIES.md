# Recovery Companies

> **Classification:** Major polity and industry family

## Identity and Material Basis

Recovery Companies are mobile salvage organizations, evacuation contractors,
heavy-lift crews, archive extractors, auction cooperatives, and industrial
fleets built around failing or contracting Domains. They turn shrinking access
windows into an economy.

## Organization and Doctrine

They range from worker-owned rescue-and-recovery cooperatives to concessionary
firms and armed liquidators. Their doctrine is that unused value lost beyond a
field boundary helps nobody; extraction must happen while routes still exist.

## What It Gets Right

Machinery, records, seed stock, and specialist tools can preserve lives
elsewhere. Mobile crews do dangerous work that settled governments postpone.

## Failure Mode

Companies can declare repairable sites doomed, strip systems that still support
residents, create dependency, manipulate condemnation, or treat evacuation as
an obstacle to a profitable timetable.

## Domain Policy

Assess recoverable value, secure access, stage extraction, and relocate assets
to places judged capable of using them.

## Custodian Relationship

Custodian credentials open the richest sealed systems and can invalidate a
salvage concession. Companies may offer unmatched logistics while fearing that
the Custodian can seize their entire operating basis without compensation.

## Environmental Grammar

Cut marks, lift frames, tagged machinery, temporary rails, modular camps,
packing grids, hazard-price boards, dismantled shells, and meticulously protected
high-value components amid rough living quarters.

## Pre-Combat and Noncombat Behavior

Crews survey, bid, isolate energy sources, dismantle equipment, load convoys,
and negotiate passage. Security defends a schedule or claim rather than hunting.

## Gameplay Opportunities

Extract machinery that permanently disables a site; race a closing route;
expose a false condemnation; choose a recoverable asset over people or archives;
defend a legitimate evacuation company from those it previously displaced.

## Internal Variation

Worker cooperatives, municipal recovery arms, family flotillas, debt-bound firms,
predatory liquidators, and rescue specialists share tools but not ethics.

## Relations

Drawdown supplies condemned zones; Fieldworks argues for repair; Charter supplies
claims; Cordon seals lucrative sites; Witness defends abandoned belongings;
Orraic Orders force rescue obligations into extraction plans.
