# Fieldworks Compact

> **Classification:** Major polity family

## Identity and Material Basis

The Fieldworks Compact is a family of anchor crews, agrarian districts, grid
engineers, pump unions, route maintainers, and settlements that cooperate to
keep continuity fields productive. It treats habitability as maintained public
work, not a natural condition.

## Organization and Doctrine

Compacts range from federated work councils to engineer-led emergency states.
They share labor, calibration data, spares, and field capacity. Their doctrine
is practical: a Domain survives when people can repair it together and use the
territory those repairs recover.

## What It Gets Right

Maintenance is political power, neglected infrastructure is a slow evacuation
order, and cautious expansion can give real futures to growing communities.

## Failure Mode

Fieldworks leaders can treat visible expansion as proof of legitimacy, reopen
unsafe territory, hide maintenance debt, or consume reserve capacity that a
Domain will need during the next shock.

## Domain Policy

Restore outer anchors, reopen farmland and routes, distribute service capacity,
and keep technical knowledge public enough for local repair.

## Custodian Relationship

Custodian access can repair systems no Compact crew can reach. It can also
bypass their safety process and make one ancient operator the deciding vote on
how much reality a population may risk.

## Environmental Grammar

Survey stakes, field-load boards, repaired conduits, modular workshops, seed
stores, marked safe radii, shared tools, temporary housing on reopened land,
and old infrastructure patched with locally standardized parts.

## Pre-Combat and Noncombat Behavior

Crews inspect anchors, escort repair teams, debate load readings, move supplies,
and warn trespassers before escalating. A hostile Compact unit defends working
infrastructure rather than wandering for targets.

## Gameplay Opportunities

Restore an outer anchor; choose usable territory versus reserve capacity;
protect a repair convoy; expose optimistic load reports; decide which reopened
service receives power first.

## Internal Variation

Expansionists, reserve stewards, mutual-aid compacts, technocratic directorates,
and isolated crews can disagree sharply without ceasing to be Fieldworks.

## Relations

Drawdown is its defining argument; Cordon slows reopening; Charter can stabilize
or privatize maintenance; Orraic Orders press it toward human rescue; Recovery
Companies tempt it to give up early; Witness Assemblies defend communities its
maps may describe only as load.
