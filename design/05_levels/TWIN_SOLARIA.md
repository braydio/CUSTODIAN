## 0. Canon lock

Twin Solaria is no longer a double-sunset observatory, solar court, sun shrine,
or literal astronomical installation.

The name **Twin Solaria** refers to two paired continuity-reference towers:
the **Outbound Solaria** and the **Reciprocal Solaria**.

The facility is the Hub's surviving **Crown Route Court**, an old-world
continuity-adjudication complex built to reconstruct, verify, judge, and, when
authorized, stabilize damaged or incomplete Lattice routes.

The decisive distinction is:

> **An ordinary Continuity Port executes a route whose address is sufficiently known.  
> Twin Solaria reconstructs whether a lost address is still a defensible route.**

The facility does not create destinations, create Domains, manufacture alternate
worlds, negate the Pale, or defeat the Reciprocal Continuity Law.

It finds and constrains continuity relationships that already exist or once existed,
then presents a Custodian with the evidence and consequences of reopening them.

The location is physically real infrastructure. It is not a simulation, dream,
Archive reconstruction, or metaphysical mind-space.

---

## 1. Why the facility exists

Old continuity infrastructure normally expected a usable route package:

- destination identity;
- receiving-side correlation;
- route geometry;
- continuity provenance;
- relay participation;
- acceptable field coherence;
- current authority;
- reciprocal-risk status.

That contract breaks after the Severing.

A route may survive only as contradictory pieces:

- one Archive lists a destination whose port no longer answers;
- a relay carries a degraded historical echo;
- an old departure register contains the destination but not the current return vector;
- two authentic records disagree about which continuity a destination belonged to;
- the physical port survives but its route index was deliberately destroyed;
- the destination still exists while every intermediate relay is gone;
- an outbound solution can be correlated but the reciprocal address is unstable;
- a route was placed on Hold and nobody alive understands why.

Twin Solaria was built for those cases.

Its job is to combine historical evidence, live field measurements, route residue,
relay echoes, provenance, and reciprocal-risk analysis into a route finding that a
Custodian can act upon.

This makes it both a machine and a court.

The machinery asks:

> Can this connection still be made?

The institution asks:

> Should this connection be allowed to exist again?

## 2. Relationship to current cosmology

Twin Solaria must obey the active Lattice Domain canon.

### 2.1 Domains are persistent places

A Lattice Domain is a damaged but existing region of post-Severing reality kept at
usable coherence by surviving Archive/Lattice infrastructure.

Twin Solaria does not generate a Domain when it finds a route.

It establishes or restores access to a place that already exists.

### 2.2 Operational distance is route distance

Twin Solaria does not care primarily about geometric distance.

A physically nearby Domain may be operationally unreachable if the route is gone.
A very distant Domain may be reachable if enough continuity relationships survive.

### 2.3 Reciprocity is absolute

Any reachable continuity may potentially establish a return path.

Twin Solaria can model, constrain, observe, throttle, and reject reciprocal exposure.

It cannot abolish reciprocity.

That is why the **Reciprocal Solaria** is as important as the Outbound Solaria.

### 2.4 Provenance is evidence

Provenance does not make a destination real.

It helps determine:

- what record belongs to what continuity;
- which route history is credible;
- whether two apparently identical destinations are actually the same operational place;
- whether a signal is local, imported, displaced, or unresolved.

### 2.5 Restoring a route is an intervention

A technically viable route is not neutral.

Reopening it changes exposure, politics, logistics, strategic access, and possibly the
survival conditions of both sides.

Twin Solaria therefore cannot be a casual fast-travel menu.

## 3. Location: the Crown Annex

### 3.1 Historical position

Before the Severing, Twin Solaria belonged to the northern crown of the Historical
City, functionally associated with Archive Heights.

Archive Heights supplied evidence, route histories, registries, observatory-grade
measurement, and adjudicative records.

Twin Solaria converted that information into live route decisions.

The old procession was conceptually:

```text
ARCHIVE HEIGHTS
    |
    | evidence / route history / authority
    v
CROWN TRANSFER
    |
    v
TWIN SOLARIA
    |
    v
CROWN APERTURE
```

### 3.2 Post-Severing position

The facility no longer sits inside ordinary continuous city traversal.

Its local section of the Hub Domain survived while portions of its surrounding
approach did not.

The result is a **detached annex**.

The player reaches it through the **Crown Transfer**, a short local continuity
handoff from Archive Heights rather than a normal street.

It is still part of the Hub.

It is not a separate Campaign Domain.

### 3.3 Why the exterior looks like outer space

The black exterior visible around the surviving platform is canonically called the
**Crown Verge**.

The Verge is not currently classified in canon as ordinary vacuum or normal outer
space.

The visual field may contain:

- ordinary stars;
- optical distortion from the annex's continuity-reference systems;
- route-side parallax;
- distant Domain light;
- reflected or refracted field structures;
- unresolved artifacts produced by looking through a damaged continuity boundary.

The game should allow the player to reasonably ask, "Is this in space?"

The game should not answer that question cheaply.

Old instrumentation does not label the exterior `SPACE`.

Appropriate machine terminology includes:

```text
EXTERIOR REFERENCE: NONLOCAL
LOCAL COHERENCE: MAINTAINED
CROWN VERGE OBSERVATION: PERMITTED
ROUTE-SIDE PARALLAX: UNRESOLVED
```

### 3.4 Physicality lock

The floor, towers, machinery, stairs, rails, and structures are physical.

The Crown Annex is not projected scenery.

A fall beyond surviving structure is dangerous.

Future authored collision should treat exposed boundaries as real edges while leaving
the exact metaphysics of the Verge unresolved.

## 4. Why it is called Twin Solaria

The facility's two namesake towers are the **Outbound Solaria** and
**Reciprocal Solaria**.

They are enormous continuity-reference anchors.

At high output, each produces a hard blue-white field glow bright enough to flood the
court with artificial daylight.

The historical nickname "Solaria" came from that operating appearance.

It is not evidence of sun worship or of two literal suns.

### 4.1 Outbound Solaria

The Outbound Solaria holds the candidate destination solution.

It answers:

- where the route is trying to go;
- whether the destination signature remains coherent;
- whether historical route records converge;
- whether the outbound aperture remains locked to the same destination during activation.

### 4.2 Reciprocal Solaria

The Reciprocal Solaria searches for the path the same connection creates back toward
the Hub.

It answers:

- what can address the Hub from the far side;
- whether the return path converges on the expected origin;
- whether unexpected reciprocal addresses appear;
- whether route activation increases return-path ambiguity;
- whether a candidate should enter Hold automatically.

### 4.3 Institutional maxim

A surviving operational maxim is:

> **A route is not acquired when the first Solaria finds the destination.  
> A route is acquired when the second finds what can come back.**

This should appear at least once in environmental text or Archive material, but not
as an omnipresent slogan.

## 5. Portal and route hierarchy

Twin Solaria must remain distinct from existing and planned traversal systems.

### 5.1 Tactical portal rings

Current procgen portal rings are local tactical traversal.

They reposition the player inside the same generated tactical map.

They are not strategic route infrastructure.

### 5.2 Gate of Dust and ordinary Continuity Ports

An ordinary Continuity Port can service a sufficiently identifiable route.

The Gate of Dust represents this class of infrastructure in the Awakening material.

A normal port expects the route problem to be largely solved already.

It can still be damaged, suspended, unsafe, unpowered, or on Hold.

### 5.3 Twin Solaria

Twin Solaria is for routes whose identity or continuity relationship is incomplete.

It can:

- reconstruct candidate route addresses;
- compare contradictory route histories;
- interrogate historical route residue;
- correlate surviving relay echoes;
- test outbound convergence;
- test reciprocal convergence;
- establish a high-order aperture once a route is authorized;
- preserve a route in a controlled observational state before full traversal;
- record why a route was authorized, held, or rejected.

### 5.4 What it cannot do

Twin Solaria cannot:

- create a new universe;
- invent a destination with no evidentiary basis;
- guarantee that an answering signal is benevolent;
- guarantee that a destination still matches its old Archive description;
- turn a destroyed Domain back into a healthy one;
- bypass the Reciprocal Continuity Law;
- erase return-path exposure after opening a route;
- casually override a Custodian Hold;
- make every old destination reachable;
- operate many crown-scale apertures simultaneously without consequence;
- serve as unrestricted teleportation for convenience.

## 7. Landmark detail

### 7.1 Crown Causeway

**Role:** arrival, decompression, first scale read.

The player should enter from the south and immediately understand that this is not a
normal Hub street.

The causeway is narrow relative to the enormous machinery ahead.

The first arrival should prioritize:

1. exposed Verge on either side;
2. the Home Index as the first readable machine;
3. the huge aperture court visible only partially at first;
4. one or more distant Solaria visible above the architecture.

The causeway should contain little clutter.

It is a threshold, not a market or habitation zone.

### 7.2 Home Index

**Role:** define "here" before attempting "there."

The Home Index is the most redundantly maintained mechanism in the complex.

Old engineers treated origin uncertainty as catastrophic.

Before Twin Solaria can reconstruct an outbound route, it establishes the Hub's local
continuity reference using independent witnesses.

The player-facing idea is simple:

> The machine must know exactly where home is before it is allowed to open a road away from it.

Environmental details:

- repeated origin marks;
- redundant calibration rails;
- duplicate authority seals;
- physical wear from repeated verification;
- several independent indicator paths converging on one center;
- damage elsewhere, but conspicuous maintenance around the index.

### 7.3 Archive Witness Ring

**Role:** historical evidence input.

This ring accepts evidence such as:

- departure registers;
- prior route geometry;
- archived relay identities;
- previous Custodian findings;
- old Holds;
- provenance chains;
- civic or Meridian route records;
- Contract history.

The ring does not declare a route true.

It says what the surviving record claims.

### 7.4 Live Witness Ring

**Role:** present-tense evidence.

This ring samples what the continuity field is doing now.

It can disagree with Archive history.

That disagreement is valuable.

A core visual principle is that both Witness Rings must remain visibly distinct and
simultaneously readable.

Twin Solaria should never visually imply that "old records" automatically override
"current behavior" or the reverse.

### 7.5 West Echo Drum

**Role:** active interrogation.

The West Echo Drum emits controlled continuity probes into a candidate route solution.

These are not weapon blasts.

They are timed diagnostic pulses.

The machine varies:

- phase;
- interval;
- field strength;
- route offset;
- expected echo timing.

The goal is to determine whether the candidate behaves like a real path rather than a
coincidental signature match.

### 7.6 East Echo Drum

**Role:** returned evidence.

The East Echo Drum listens for:

- expected destination response;
- reflection;
- duplicate responses;
- delayed return;
- unexpected reciprocal addresses;
- phase behavior inconsistent with the historical route.

The two drums operate as a paired diagnostic system.

They should never be framed as artillery.

### 7.7 Meridian Needle

**Role:** candidate reconstruction.

The Meridian Needle is the facility's long-baseline correlator.

It combines Archive history, Witness data, and Echo response into a route candidate.

The term **Meridian** refers to continuity-route reference, not planetary longitude.

The Needle is not itself the gate.

It determines the solution that the rest of the facility may attempt to hold.

### 7.8 Reciprocity Dial

**Role:** evidence made actionable.

The Reciprocity Dial is the emotional center of the level.

It presents the relationship between:

- outbound convergence;
- return-path convergence;
- field strain;
- destination confidence;
- provenance conflict;
- known Holds;
- unresolved reciprocal addresses.

The Dial is where the machine stops being merely impressive and becomes morally
dangerous.

A route can be:

- technically reachable;
- strategically useful;
- historically important;
- and still unacceptable to open.

### 7.9 Authority Threshold

**Role:** institutional brake.

The monumental threshold between the analysis courts and the aperture court is
deliberate.

No one should accidentally wander from "we found a route" into "we opened a route."

The threshold is where recognized Custodian authority changes the state from analysis
to intervention.

Appropriate threshold results:

```text
CANDIDATE CORRELATED
RECIPROCAL REVIEW REQUIRED
CUSTODIAN AUTHORITY: PRESENT
```

or:

```text
ROUTE VIABLE
RECIPROCAL EXPOSURE: UNRESOLVED
RESTORATION AUTHORITY: WITHHELD
```

### 7.10 Outbound Solaria

**Role:** destination lock.

Once authorized for acquisition, the Outbound Solaria holds the destination solution
stable while the Crown Aperture begins resolving.

Its visual response should be clean and narrow.

A stable Outbound Solaria is not proof that the route is safe.

### 7.11 Reciprocal Solaria

**Role:** return-path lock.

The Reciprocal Solaria is deliberately allowed to become visually more alarming than
the Outbound Solaria.

A route that looks perfect outbound can still produce unstable or multiple return
solutions.

If the Reciprocal Solaria fails to converge, the default state is Hold or shutdown.

### 7.12 Coherence Witnesses

**Role:** distributed fail-closed instrumentation.

The smaller blue crystal fixtures throughout the complex form a physical consensus
network.

They monitor local field stress and verify that one damaged central instrument is not
lying to the rest of the court.

Presentation rule:

- calm agreement: dim, synchronized pulses;
- rising disagreement: asynchronous pulse timing;
- route instability: local witness dropouts;
- emergency shutdown: witnesses go dark in a cascading pattern.

### 7.13 Blind Witness

**Role:** optional lore and restoration node.

The detached left-side tower is canonically a severed auxiliary witness.

It survived physically but was intentionally isolated from the court.

Its damage should read differently from random ruin:

- cut trunk lines;
- blackened isolation hardware;
- manual separation marks;
- surviving local status plate;
- no indication that simple age alone caused the disconnect.

The reason for the disconnection remains open.

Possible later discoveries can establish whether it was:

- precaution;
- sabotage;
- an emergency response;
- an attempt to stop false consensus;
- a deliberate way to blind the court to a specific route behavior.

Do not resolve this in the baseline location spec.

### 7.14 Crown Aperture

**Role:** high-order route manifestation.

The aperture occupies the enormous upper-left circular court.

The whole circular structure is part of the machine.

The visible opening is only the most obvious component.

The asymmetrical placement is intentional safety architecture.

If something returns through the Crown Aperture, it does not emerge on a direct line
toward:

- the Home Index;
- the primary controls;
- the Crown Causeway;
- Archive Heights;
- the main Hub.

The court provides lateral distance, containment geometry, and time to react.

The aperture can show a destination before full traversal is authorized.

That image is a **Resolved Route Vista**.

It is not a literal window in the ordinary optical sense.

## 8. Interpreting the current golden vista

The golden landscape currently visible through the Crown Aperture is canonical as a
**Resolved Route Vista**.

It is not the Crown Annex's sunrise.

It is not one of two local suns.

It is not evidence that Twin Solaria is built on a planet with strange astronomy.

The current composite therefore depicts the facility in one of two acceptable states:

1. **ROUTE CANDIDATE / observational resolve**, where the destination is visually
   correlated but traversal is not yet authorized; or
2. **STABLE**, where a route has been fully authorized and held.

For baseline documentation, prefer interpretation **1**.

That preserves dramatic uncertainty in the reference image.

## 9. Operational sequence

Twin Solaria uses a fail-closed progression.

### 9.1 DORMANT

Conditions:

- Crown Aperture closed;
- Solaria dim;
- Echo Drums inert;
- Witness Rings readable only as dead machinery;
- Coherence Witnesses mostly dark.

### 9.2 AUTHORITY WAKE

Trigger:

- recognized Custodian designation enters the court or reactivates the Home Index.

Effects:

- basic local systems acknowledge authority;
- Coherence Witnesses begin a low-power census;
- no route is selected;
- no aperture forms.

### 9.3 HOME REFERENCE

The Home Index establishes the current Hub origin.

Failure here blocks all further route work.

Possible outputs:

```text
HOME REFERENCE: CONVERGED
LOCAL DOMAIN: CORRELATED
RECIPROCAL ORIGIN: STABLE
```

or:

```text
HOME REFERENCE: DEGRADED
ROUTE WORK: PROHIBITED
```

### 9.4 LISTENING

The facility passively surveys known route residue.

No active interrogation yet.

The player may see a list of:

- historical route names;
- confidence;
- last known status;
- Hold state;
- current response.

### 9.5 CORRELATING

Archive Witness, Live Witness, and Meridian Needle begin active comparison.

A route may fail here because the evidence does not converge.

### 9.6 ROUTE CANDIDATE

The machine has enough evidence to produce a candidate destination solution.

Important:

**candidate does not mean safe.**

The Crown Aperture may begin a faint observational resolve.

### 9.7 RECIPROCITY REVIEW

The Reciprocal Solaria and East Echo Drum determine what return path the candidate
creates.

Possible machine outcomes include:

```text
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: CONVERGED
```

```text
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: MULTIPLE
```

```text
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: NONCONVERGENT
```

```text
RETURN-PATH CONTACT: UNRESOLVED
```

### 9.8 CUSTODIAN DECISION

Available high-level findings:

- **HOLD**
- **ABORT**
- **AUTHORIZE RESTORATION**

A Hold preserves the evidence and prevents activation while uncertainty remains.

Abort rejects the current attempt without claiming the route can never be revisited.

Authorize Restoration permits the system to proceed to aperture resolve.

### 9.9 APERTURE RESOLVE

The Crown Aperture transitions from route sample to traversable continuity interface.

This should be slow enough to read as industrial work, not a spell.

### 9.10 STABLE

The route is open under monitored conditions.

All major instruments remain active.

The facility continuously rechecks:

- destination lock;
- return path;
- field strain;
- witness agreement.

### 9.11 SHUTDOWN

Normal closure collapses the aperture under control.

The route record remains.

The facility records:

- who authorized;
- what evidence supported it;
- any anomalies;
- final reciprocal state;
- reason for closure.

### 9.12 RECIPROCAL WARNING

A route can degrade after opening.

Warning conditions may include:

- new return address;
- witness disagreement;
- unexpected echo;
- route drift;
- Home Index instability;
- sudden field load.

The default response is throttle and Hold.

### 9.13 EMERGENCY CLOSURE

If the route crosses a safety threshold, Twin Solaria closes it without waiting for
a new discretionary command.

A Custodian may own the decision to open.

The machine is allowed to fail closed.

## 10. Custodian authority

Twin Solaria exists to make Custodian authority mechanically and narratively specific.

The player is not special because only they can press a mystical button.

The player is special because old infrastructure recognizes their designation as a
surviving continuity jurisdiction.

Twin Solaria can gather evidence without the player.

It cannot convert unresolved evidence into a discretionary route restoration without
the authority the Custodian office was designed to provide.

Relevant powers:

- inspection;
- attestation;
- intervention;
- Hold;
- adjudication.

### 10.1 Key gameplay principle

**The machine determines possibility.  
The player determines permissibility.**

That distinction must survive any future UI implementation.

## 11. Gameplay role across progression

### 11.1 Not the first Contract

The first Contract remains associated with the later Hub/Forum sequence and an
ordinary Continuity Port.

Twin Solaria is a later escalation.

This protects pacing.

The player should understand ordinary route infrastructure before being introduced to
the machine used when ordinary route infrastructure is no longer enough.

### 11.2 First Twin Solaria visit

Recommended state:

- Crown Annex reachable;
- Home Index partly active;
- most Witnesses dormant;
- one Solaria capable of limited response;
- Crown Aperture inert;
- Blind Witness isolated;
- no strategic route restoration yet.

The visit establishes scale, authority recognition, and danger.

### 11.3 First restoration arc

The player recovers enough evidence to reconstruct one lost route.

This teaches:

- route evidence can be incomplete;
- "reachable" is not the same as "authorized";
- reciprocity can introduce new risk;
- a Hold is an active decision, not failure.

### 11.4 Mature strategic use

Twin Solaria becomes the location for exceptional routes such as:

- a Domain whose original Continuity Port is dead;
- an old Custodian Post reachable only through partial records;
- a destination another authority deliberately severed;
- a route known from contradictory Archive evidence;
- a strategic Domain whose reciprocal signature is politically dangerous;
- a route that exposes a surviving population to outside access;
- a route connected to continuity-displaced or adjacent phenomena.

### 11.5 Late-game use

Late-game Twin Solaria decisions should force the player to weigh:

- evidence quality;
- reciprocal exposure;
- local Domain survival;
- strategic value;
- political consequences;
- historical obligation;
- the cost of leaving a route closed.

The system should never collapse into "green route good, red route bad."

## 12. Decision grammar

A Twin Solaria decision should provide enough evidence to support judgment without
pretending certainty.

A route review should expose categories such as:

- **OUTBOUND CORRELATION**
- **RECIPROCAL CORRELATION**
- **DESTINATION CONFIDENCE**
- **PROVENANCE CONFLICT**
- **KNOWN HOLD**
- **FIELD LOAD**
- **RETURN-PATH CONTACT**
- **LOCAL IMPACT**
- **RECEIVING AUTHORITY**
- **EVIDENCE GAPS**

The game may show explicit numbers internally, but player-facing presentation should
prefer classifications unless precision materially matters.

Recommended vocabulary:

```text
CONVERGED
DEGRADED
CONFLICT
UNRESOLVED
MULTIPLE
STABLE
RISING
WITHHELD
AUTHORIZED
HELD
```

## 13. Example route findings

### 13.1 Safe-looking but historically held

```text
ROUTE CANDIDATE: CORRELATED
OUTBOUND CONVERGENCE: HIGH
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: CONVERGED
HISTORICAL HOLD: ACTIVE
HOLD BASIS: SEALED RECORD
RESTORATION AUTHORITY: WITHHELD
```

The challenge is not engineering.

It is whether the player opens the sealed evidence first.

### 13.2 Valuable but reciprocal ambiguity

```text
DESTINATION: CORRELATED
RESOURCE / CIVIL INFRASTRUCTURE: PRESENT
OUTBOUND CONVERGENCE: ACCEPTABLE
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: MULTIPLE
RETURN-PATH CONTACT: UNRESOLVED
```

### 13.3 Old record no longer matches reality

```text
ARCHIVE DESTINATION: VERIFIED
LIVE WITNESS: CONFLICT
ROUTE GEOMETRY: CHANGED
RECEIVING AUTHORITY: NONE
```

### 13.4 Route that answers too well

```text
ROUTE CANDIDATE: CORRELATED
RESPONSE LATENCY: BELOW HISTORICAL MINIMUM
RECIPROCAL ADDRESS: CONVERGED
UNSOLICITED RETURN TRAFFIC: DETECTED
```

Do not automatically explain why.

## 14. Environmental storytelling

### 14.1 The Home Index was protected more carefully than the gate

The facility should visibly communicate that the builders feared origin confusion more
than spectacle.

### 14.2 Holds remain physically present

Historical Holds should survive as material traces:

- locked indicator rails;
- sealed plates;
- dead authority lamps;
- physically interrupted command paths;
- Archive entries attached to specific route records.

### 14.3 The facility was used during emergencies

Signs should include:

- hurried manual bypasses;
- temporary cable runs;
- patched witness housings;
- one or two emergency inscriptions;
- locked-down side mechanisms;
- maintenance performed unevenly.

### 14.4 The Blind Witness was deliberately isolated

The player should be able to distinguish "broken by age" from "cut on purpose."

### 14.5 The aperture court is defensively offset

Architecture itself teaches reciprocal risk.

The gate is not aligned directly with the city.

### 14.6 No habitation

Twin Solaria is not a palace.

It may have service recesses and old operator stations, but no cozy living district.

The level should feel like a cathedral built for a dangerous civil machine.

## 15. Visual language

### 15.1 Materials

Preserve the existing reference language:

- cold stone;
- blackened civic metal;
- brass/gold trim;
- dark iron;
- blue-white crystalline continuity instruments;
- selective enamel;
- very limited warm light.

### 15.2 The crystals

The crystalline structures are technical continuity-field components.

Avoid generic magical crystal behavior.

Their motion language should be:

- narrow internal line movement;
- controlled pulses;
- phase shifts;
- coherent brightening;
- desynchronization during faults.

Avoid:

- rainbow sparkle;
- loose particle fountains;
- fantasy rune spam;
- constant idle fireworks.

### 15.3 Crown Aperture

The aperture should look like a destination being **resolved**, not a flat television.

Useful layers:

1. structural ring;
2. field edge;
3. remote image;
4. subtle parallax disagreement;
5. local distortion;
6. sparse route-registration marks.

The remote vista should remain readable.

Do not bury it under VFX.

### 15.4 Crown Verge

The Verge should remain quiet enough that the court reads clearly.

Motion should be slow and ambiguous.

The space-like appearance is desirable.

Literal astronomical explanation is not.

### 15.5 Color hierarchy

Baseline:

- stone/iron: low saturation;
- brass: restrained warm authority color;
- continuity instruments: blue/cyan;
- active destination resolve: destination-derived color;
- warnings: use existing Black Reliquary / institutional warning language rather than
  turning the whole scene red.

## 16. Lighting

### Dormant

- low environmental light;
- weak witness points;
- no active aperture light;
- court reads primarily by silhouette and surviving local fixtures.

### Correlating

- Home Index becomes a stable visual anchor;
- Witness Rings gain narrow illumination;
- Meridian Needle begins controlled blue-white output;
- drums receive brief pulse light only when active.

### Reciprocity Review

- both Solaria active;
- their relationship should be visually legible;
- subtle disagreement in pulse timing communicates uncertainty before UI text does.

### Stable aperture

- remote destination light enters the court;
- local materials catch that destination's hue;
- this is the main way the current golden vista can flood the left court.

### Warning

- do not globally strobe the map;
- local witness disagreement and Reciprocal Solaria instability should carry the first
  warning;
- aperture distortion escalates only after sustained risk.

---

## 17. Audio

Twin Solaria needs a machine identity distinct from ordinary portals.

### Dormant bed

- very low infrastructure resonance;
- sparse metal cooling;
- occasional far relay tick;
- Verge nearly silent.

### Home Index

- steady low-frequency lock tone;
- no triumphant chord.

### Echo Drums

- paired impulses with different roles;
- West: controlled emission;
- East: delayed returned response.

The player should learn to hear when the East return is wrong.

### Solaria

Outbound and Reciprocal should occupy related but distinct tonal bands.

Stable operation produces consonance.

Risk produces beat-frequency disagreement, not generic alarm sirens.

### Crown Aperture

The sound should feel like environmental pressure resolving into geometry.

Avoid "whoosh portal" as the main identity.

### Emergency closure

Closure should be fast, physical, and final.

A short silence immediately afterward is valuable.

## 18. Camera and reveal grammar

### Reveal A: Crown Causeway

Purpose: establish exposure and scale.

Frame:

- player small;
- Home Index ahead;
- Verge visible on both sides;
- upper machinery partially occluded.

### Reveal B: Home Index

Purpose: teach the origin-first logic.

Camera should briefly show that multiple lines and mechanisms converge inward.

### Reveal C: Reciprocity Court

Purpose: show Echo Drums, Dial, and paired Solaria as one system.

This is the "understand the machine" vista.

### Reveal D: Crown Aperture

Purpose: destination spectacle.

Do not reveal the full aperture immediately on first arrival if avoidable.

The upper-left placement supports a delayed lateral reveal.

### Reveal E: Active route

When a remote vista resolves, camera framing should keep enough local architecture in
view that the player understands they are standing at an aperture, not viewing a
cutscene background.

The current backdrop preview has perimeter collision only.

## 20. Interaction plan

Baseline interactables should be sparse.

Recommended:

1. **Home Index console**
   - establishes local reference;
   - always relevant.

   - route history and old Holds.

3. **Live Witness terminal**
   - current field evidence.

4. **Reciprocity Dial**
   - primary decision surface.

5. **Blind Witness**
   - optional repair / lore investigation.

6. **Authority Threshold**

Traversal should be allowed only once route state and authority already permit it.

## 21. Faction and political consequences

Twin Solaria is strategically terrifying because it can restore access that modern
societies assumed was permanently gone.

Possible consequences of a route decision include:

- exposing a Domain to outside actors;
- restoring trade;
- enabling evacuation;
- enabling military movement;
- reopening a disputed historical jurisdiction;
- invalidating a faction's monopoly on access;
- revealing that an allegedly destroyed community still exists;
- restoring a route that another polity deliberately kept closed;
- proving that an old Hold had a valid reason;
- proving that a famous prohibition was based on bad evidence.

The facility itself should remain Custodian institutional space.

Do not convert it into a faction headquarters.

## 30. Future runtime ownership

When implemented, prefer existing authored-level and route authorities.

Expected integration shape:

- authored Twin Solaria scene owns local collision, landmarks, camera zones, and
  interactable placement;
- route-state gameplay should have one focused authority rather than being embedded

- existing route traversal infrastructure owns actual scene/world handoff;

`ui.gd`, or an oversized level script.

## 34. Design thesis

Twin Solaria should make one idea physical:

> **Opening a road is not the same thing as proving a road should be open.**

The player walks through the logic of that decision.

They enter by establishing home.

They compare memory to present evidence.

They interrogate the candidate.

They see the return path.

Only then do they stand before the aperture.

That is the Crown Route Court.
