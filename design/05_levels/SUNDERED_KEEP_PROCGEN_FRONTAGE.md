# Sundered Keep Procgen Frontage

- **Status:** production authority — generated frontage and distant reveal
- **Owner:** procgen world intent and Sundered Keep frontage runtime
- **Runtime:** `custodian/` Godot 4.x
- **Last updated:** 2026-07-31

## Production Boundary

Production flows through three distinct ownership domains:

```text
generated campaign world and Sundered Keep frontage
-> short ordinary fade
-> authored Shore Parish / Outer Wall Approach
-> short ordinary fade
-> Sundered Keep Front Gate
```

This document owns the generated-world frontage, its floor/collision/dressing
authority, the distant Keep reveal, and the terminal ingress into the Parish.
`SUNDERED_KEEP_VISTA_APPROACH.md` owns the authored Parish and close checkpoint
detail. `SUNDERED_KEEP_LARGE_FRONT_GATE.md` owns the first full Keep map.

There is no playable blackout corridor, authored procgen route-master floor,
or second procgen camera pull in the production route.

## Authority

The Sundered Keep frontage is generated by the world intent graph. No authored
route-master sprite or rectangular special-room footprint owns its playable
floor.

Authored assets own only landmark identity, architectural modules, parallax
presentation, fog, reversible camera rhythm, and gate semantics.

The production frontage flow is:

```text
generated campaign world
→ generated Sundered Keep approach/frontage
→ ordinary fade into the authored Shore Parish
```

Entering or backing out of the frontage does not load a scene, fade, teleport,
or disable procgen. `WorldIngressSite` begins the production route only at the
generated terminal threshold, where a normal fade stages the Parish.

## Ownership

`ASCENT_FIELD` remains the substrate and pipeline authority:

1. world profile;
2. intent graph and ascent spine;
3. Sundered Keep landmark intent;
4. irregular frontage mask and semantic reservations;
5. route playability;
6. terrain and elevation;
7. floor, wall, cliff, and collision generation;
8. sites;
9. props, foliage, actors, and presentation;
10. final validation.

The frontage is not a `SpecialRoomRuntimeInserter` site and never calls
`claim_procgen_floor_rect_for_authored_scene_tiles()`. Its internal iteration
bounds are an implementation detail; only cells selected by route, terrace,
side-pocket, fortress-exclusion, and seeded edge masks receive authority.

Procgen owns floor, walls, cliffs, elevation requests, collision, navigation
clearance, dressing rejection, encounters, and minimap cells. Presentation
owns no playable-ground or collision authority.

## V1 Grammar

V1 implements one complete semantic grammar:

```text
main route
→ rising approach
→ first overlook
→ curved outer-wall traverse
→ one side pocket
→ gate compression
→ Front Gate threshold
```

The world seed varies centerline curvature, terrace fields, cliff breakup,
overlook position, side-pocket side/size, encounter pocket, and secondary ruin
anchors. The destination identity, reveal beats, frontage beat, compression,
and reachable terminal gate remain stable.

The result is exported through the existing procgen level-data contract as
`sundered_keep_frontage`. It contains:

## Shape And Safety Contract

- Centerline brushes provide a minimum seven-cell post-dressing combat route.
- Hard centerline clearance cannot be eroded or dressed with blockers.
- Terrace and side-pocket fields are ellipses/metaball-like masks, not cleared
  rectangles.
- Seeded edge erosion is permitted only outside hard clearance.
- Fortress exclusion cells prevent unrelated mandatory sites from occupying
  the frontage identity zone.
- Cliff cells border selected non-route edges and use ordinary ascent-field
  blocker authority.
- The gate anchor, route anchors, and reveal anchors are required cells for
  terrain connectivity.
- The generated terminal ingress is exactly at `gate_anchor`.

## Presentation And Camera

Production presentation is spawned from semantic anchors exported by the
frontage. The old authored approach remains a composition reference.

The one procgen camera envelope is sampled by normalized distance along the
generated centerline:

```text
0.00  frontage entry
0.18  first influence begins
0.32  first reveal apex
0.46  first return complete
1.00  Parish ingress threshold
```

The reveal derives continuously from Operator position. The shared Camera2D
retains authority, movement is never locked, and presentation framing releases
outside the envelope, on reversal, on departure from the semantic corridor,
and before route transition. The focal point remains Operator-relative so the
Operator stays readable in the lower quarter of the frame. `frontage_weight`
may control visual alpha but never camera position, zoom, offset, follow target,
or bounds. Visual coverage at cinematic zoom must resolve
to generated terrain/cliff, fortress modules, storm/ocean underlay, fog, or
foreground occlusion—never clear color.

The distant Keep, storm horizon, veil/fog, fortress planes, foreground ruins,
and gate-shadow treatment attach to semantic anchors. Large sprites may cover
non-playable background space but never define traversal or collision.

## Validation

Run from `custodian/`:

```bash
env HOME=/tmp/custodian-godot-home godot --headless --path . \
  --script res://tools/validation/sundered_keep_procgen_frontage_smoke.gd
env HOME=/tmp/custodian-godot-home godot --headless --path . \
  --script res://tools/validation/procgen_worldgen_shape_smoke.gd
env HOME=/tmp/custodian-godot-home godot --headless --path . \
  --script res://tools/validation/sundered_keep_ingress_smoke.gd
bash tools/validation/run_procgen_validation_suite.sh
```

The focused smoke reviews multiple seeds for singular intent, route
attachment/reachability/width, non-rectangular authority, special-room and
route-master exclusion, terminal ingress placement, dressing clearance,
ordered camera anchors, reverse connectivity, determinism, and meaningful
variation.

Renderer review writes eight 2560×1440 seed sets plus a manifest under:

```text
reports/sundered_keep_procgen_frontage/
```

The completed review covers eight seeds and all 32 required frames. The
manifest records deterministic semantic output, seed variation, and the
absence of authored-pocket or rectangular-footprint authority. The production
renderer review—not the legacy authored-approach Moment Forge scenario—is the
visual acceptance artifact for this system.

## Acceptance

- The generated world remains active until the Parish ingress threshold.
- No authored rectangle or route-master image owns playable frontage ground.
- The footprint is irregular and varies meaningfully by seed.
- Terrain, collision, minimap, sites, and dressing consume procgen authority.
- Exactly one procgen camera reveal works in both travel directions and always
  restores Operator follow.
- Supported cinematic framing exposes no undressed or clear-color region.
- Existing procgen, terrain, route, minimap, and ingress smokes pass.
