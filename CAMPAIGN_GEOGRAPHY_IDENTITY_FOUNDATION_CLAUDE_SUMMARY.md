# F15-B1 · Persistent Campaign Geography Identity Foundation

## Outcome

Implemented the approved data-only F15-B1 identity foundation in the claimed workstream. `CampaignGeographyRegistry` registers bounded finite Domain definitions and resolves defensive copies of canonical Domain/Location descriptors. `CampaignVisitReference` records distinct visits against the same persistent Domain without taking over `CampaignSession` lifecycle.

The registry enforces the F14-compatible ASCII ID grammar (1–64 characters), bounded seeds and topology, schema 1, and the explicitly supported `geo-v1` generation version. Canonical address keys length-prefix UTF-8 byte counts for each component. Locality provenance is SHA-256-derived from Domain seed, Domain ID, Location ID, and generation version; it does not use randomized process hashes, scene IDs, or visit IDs. Locations and adjacency are normalized into stable order. Undirected edges require exactly two reciprocal mappings under one edge ID; directed transit must be explicitly marked. Registration validates a complete candidate before adding any state, and all resolution returns deep copies.

Two fixed-seed fixture Domains, `domain.alpha` (12345) and `domain.beta` (54321), each use two synthetic sites and an explicit reciprocal road. Two distinct visits to `domain.alpha` resolve identical location provenance and adjacency while retaining different visit IDs. The smoke also checks dotted-ID key collisions, invalid and duplicate identities, unsupported schema/generation, invalid seed provenance, unknown endpoints, reciprocal-edge failures, route/level identity separation, defensive copies, failed-visit atomicity, and absence of gameplay Node creation.

No route/session integration, physical terrain, procgen adapter, actor/residency handoff, disk persistence, autoload, world-map UI, or continuous travel was added. F02, F06, F14, and REMAP-3 ownership remains unchanged. The approved D1–D7 record now describes the shipped B1 boundary; `CURRENT_STATE.md` explicitly leaves residency and traversal unimplemented.
