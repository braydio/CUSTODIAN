## Why collision and elevation are broken

The invisible/nonmatching walls are structural problems with this procedural builder.

Every wall sprite is rendered with a visual offset of `-32 px`, but its rectangular collision is created on the original unshifted tile:

```gdscript
_add_sprite(..., Vector2(0.0, -32.0))
_add_blocker(Rect2i(tile, Vector2i.ONE), ...)
```

That means the visible wall and its collider are not guaranteed to occupy the same place. It naturally produces both:

- visible wall areas that do not block;
- invisible blocking areas below the displayed wall.

The system also:

- creates rectangular collision across every ocean-hole region;
- gives every “blocking” prop a full one-tile square collider;
- places huge rectangular blockers around map boundaries;
- creates long rectangular cliff and gate blockers.

The apparently floating Operator comes from a fake elevation system. It assigns areas heights `0–3` and moves the Operator’s rendered elevation by:

```gdscript
height * 24 px
```

That happens independently of whether the displayed architecture convincingly represents that elevation.
