## Canon locked

- Custodian authority is system-bound, not territorially sovereign.
- Five mandate powers: Inspection, Attestation, Intervention, Hold, Adjudication.
- The mandate follows the continuity problem and expires with exceptional jurisdiction.
- Custodian doctrine prefers preserved comparison and reversible action under uncertainty.
- The Civic Mesh supplies evidence relationships; Custodians issue accountable operational judgments.
- Designations are persistent institutional identities associated with Posts and do not prove personal continuity.
- Crèches return a recognized designation to operational service without resolving biological/personhood ambiguity.
- A Post is an assigned sphere of continuity responsibility, making RETURN TO POST an obligation rather than a room destination.
- The Hub supports judgment; it does not supersede the Custodian as accountable decision-maker.
- Contracts both authorize and constrain extraordinary intervention.
- Meridian Office is a separate regional civil continuity operator.
- Null Warrant is a separate containment authority. Either Custodian or Null Warrant jurisdiction may block an opening; neither casually overrides the other's valid restriction.
- The office's institutional failure mode is faith in adjudication under uncertainty, not generic corruption.
