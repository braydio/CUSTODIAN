## Vigil ready-to-fast E/W identity correction (required F2 acceptance, 2026-10-23)

The selector correctly checks exact then same-action south only and reports a missing animation otherwise. `operator_runtime_manifest.generated.json` and the generated `operator_runtime_frames.tres` contain **only east/west** identities for `melee_1h/transition/idle_fast_transition_01/{e,w}/{lower_body,upper_body}` and `melee_1h_dagger/transition/idle_fast_transition_01/{e,w}/weapon`.
