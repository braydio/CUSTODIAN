# CUSTODIAN STRUCTURE DESIGN CONTRACT

**Status:** proposed lock
**Scope:** all interactable and environmental man-made structures in CUSTODIAN
**Purpose:** define a stable descriptive language for structures so future asset creation stays in-theme, readable, and production-usable.

---

## 1. Core Thesis

Custodian structures are:

* **industrial**
* **fortified**
* **weathered**
* **functional**
* **ritual-adjacent, but not ceremonial**

They are **not** sleek consumer sci-fi, not fantasy cathedral props, and not noisy dieselpunk junk piles.

The world should read as:

> a ruined authority that built durable field infrastructure for survival, production, control, and passage.

That means every structure should feel like it was **built to do a job first**, and only secondarily carries faction identity.

---

## 2. Absolute Style Pillars

Every Custodian structure should satisfy these 5 pillars.

### A. Functional First

The player should be able to guess what the object *does* from its shape.

Examples:

* fabrication structure should have a chamber, aperture, output bay, console, tool head, or loading face
* power structure should have coils, tanks, conduits, vents, or transformer-like logic
* ingress/transport structure should have a clear central focal ring, emitter, platform, or gateway

If the player cannot infer a function, the design has drifted into decorative concept art.

---

### B. Fortified Utility

Custodian structures are **hard-edged and defensible**.

Common form language:

* thick walls
* armored housings
* buttressed sides
* inset recesses
* framed apertures
* reinforced corners
* plated access panels
* mechanical brackets
* retaining rings
* protected lights, not exposed glassy luxury surfaces

They should feel like they belong in a dangerous world.

---

### C. Ruined Authority

The structures should imply a once-organized institutional power.

That means:

* repeated construction grammar
* consistent insignia placement
* disciplined silhouettes
* intentional component hierarchy
* not improvised scrap heaps

Wear and decay are welcome, but the underlying design should still feel **engineered** rather than chaotic.

---

### D. Restrained Mysticism

Custodian can have holy-tech or reliquary undertones, but they must remain **subordinate to utility**.

Allowed:

* subtle insignia
* circular seals
* controlled teal illumination
* austere heraldic panels
* grave institutional symbolism

Not allowed:

* giant altar energy theatrics
* overt shrine staging
* candles / relic clutter everywhere
* fantasy temple dominance
* ornament overpowering function

---

### E. Readability at Gameplay Scale

The object must survive being reduced to an in-game structure sprite.

That means:

* strong silhouette
* 1–2 clear focal features
* limited clutter
* readable interaction face
* readable lit vs unlit state
* distinguishable faction identity from a distance

If it only looks good at concept-art zoom, it fails.

---

# 3. Silhouette Contract

## 3.1 Base silhouette rule

Every structure needs a **clear primary mass**, a **secondary support mass**, and at most **1 tertiary accent cluster**.

Example:

* primary mass: fabrication chamber
* secondary mass: utility/power housing
* tertiary accent: side tank or antenna

Do not build every structure as a full ring of equally-detailed junk.

---

## 3.2 Structure should read from 3 distances

### Long range

The player should understand:

* "this is a structure"
* "this belongs to Custodian"
* "this is interactable / important / industrial"

### Mid range

The player should understand:

* the front
* the likely interaction side
* the structure's class

### Close range

The player should see:

* panels
* wear
* vents
* clamps
* cables
* insignia
* state lights

---

## 3.3 Shape vocabulary

Preferred silhouettes:

* squat industrial blocks
* ring-chamber with front access
* compact utility towers
* anchored modules
* reinforced pads with housed cores
* heavy box + chamber + side support cluster

Avoid:

* spindly fragile shapes
* ultra-tall tower-dominant silhouettes for common utility structures
* over-wide cathedral footprints
* pure circles with no readable frontage
* giant smooth monoliths with no function cues

---

# 4. Material Contract

## 4.1 Primary materials

Custodian structures should primarily use:

* dark steel
* blackened iron
* cold stone-metal composite
* weathered gray-blue metal
* gunmetal
* worn alloy
* occasional bronze / brass trim

These are the **default** materials.

---

## 4.2 Material finish

Surface finish should be:

* matte to semi-matte
* chipped
* scuffed
* stained
* slightly corroded
* grime-settled in recesses

Avoid:

* glossy polished luxury finish
* chrome
* clean white sterile sci-fi shell
* cartoon rust overload
* random rainbow metals

---

## 4.3 Structural layering

Good structure surfaces have layers like:

* outer plate
* recessed seam
* inner brace
* vent/grill
* protected light strip
* anchored foot/base

This layered construction is key to the Custodian feel.

---

# 5. Color Contract

## 5.1 Base palette

Primary structure colors:

* charcoal
* slate gray
* cold steel blue-gray
* dark ash
* desaturated olive-gray in rare soft materials / tarps

Secondary accents:

* brass
* muted bronze
* worn gold only in small amounts

Energy / signal accent:

* restrained teal / cyan

---

## 5.2 Accent ratio

Use this approximate ratio:

* **80–88%** dark neutrals
* **8–15%** metal accent / trim / grime variation
* **2–6%** emissive teal/cyan

The glow should feel precious and controlled, not like a nightclub.

---

## 5.3 Color rejection rules

Do not use:

* bright saturated red as a base design language
* vivid neon green as a core accent
* purple core glow for ordinary Custodian infrastructure
* candy-blue sci-fi surfaces
* gold everywhere

---

# 6. Lighting Contract

## 6.1 Emissive usage

Lights must indicate:

* power
* active state
* operational chamber
* console readability
* status strips
* signal mast

Lights should not exist just to make the asset look cooler.

---

## 6.2 Emissive placement

Preferred:

* inside chambers
* in narrow slits
* in status bars
* under protected glass
* in recessed consoles
* in compact beacons

Avoid:

* giant glowing outlines
* full-edge neon tracing
* too many separate glows
* magical aura clouds around ordinary machines

---

## 6.3 State readability

Every important structure should have at least:

* **unpowered state**
* **idle powered state**
* **active / working state**

These should be visually distinct mainly through lighting and small mechanism changes, not by changing the whole design.

---

# 7. Construction Grammar

Every Custodian structure should be assembled from a repeatable visual grammar.

## 7.1 Approved parts language

Use combinations of:

* ring chambers
* armored box housings
* reinforced pylons
* pipe elbows
* vent stacks
* ribbed conduits
* front ramps
* service lips
* inset consoles
* side tanks
* panel latches
* braces
* clamps
* feet / stabilizers
* guarded beacons
* controlled insignia plates

---

## 7.2 Restricted detail types

Allowed in moderation:

* tarped crate-like attachments
* cable bundles
* small fans
* utility canisters
* compact signal antennas

Not allowed as dominant design:

* skull spam
* giant banners on all structures
* church windows
* ornate filigree
* huge ceremonial symbols
* random protrusions with no purpose

---

## 7.3 Insignia rule

Faction insignia should be:

* present
* small
* deliberate
* stamped or mounted

Good:

* one front plate
* one side tank
* one banner on a major structure

Bad:

* insignia on every panel face

---

# 8. Frontage / Interaction Contract

This is critical.

Every interactable structure must have a **readable interaction face**.

## Required interaction cues

At least 2 of the following:

* front ramp
* service lip
* output tray
* console
* access hatch
* bay opening
* central interaction plate
* inward-facing chamber opening

The player should know where to approach without needing trial and error.

---

# 9. Structure Classes

Use these classes to keep assets coherent.

## Class A: Utility Nodes

Examples:

* fabricators
* power units
* small relays
* field terminals

Traits:

* compact
* clear front
* readable state
* strong gameplay silhouette

---

## Class B: Transit / Ingress Structures

Examples:

* gates
* portal assemblies
* ingress sites
* transfer pads

Traits:

* central focal geometry
* destination logic
* ceremonial undertone acceptable
* more spatially important than Class A

---

## Class C: Defensive / Authority Structures

Examples:

* wall adjuncts
* checkpoint systems
* fortified turrets
* control stations

Traits:

* more fortified
* less delicate
* stronger frontal authority

---

## Class D: Major Strategic Structures

Examples:

* sanctum forges
* reliquary foundries
* compound hubs

Traits:

* larger
* more symbolic
* may borrow more from shrine/authority language
* but still function-first

---

# 10. Hard Rejection Rules

If a structure hits these, reject and redo.

## Reject if it reads as:

* generic polished sci-fi machine
* fantasy altar
* random scrap pile
* oversized monument
* UI icon instead of world object
* over-detailed concept blob
* too soft / rounded / toy-like
* too many lit elements
* unclear interaction direction
* function not inferable

---

# 11. Anchor Example: Compound Fabricator

This is the baseline example for the contract.

## 11.1 Design identity

The **Compound Fabricator** is a **Class A Utility Node**.

It should read as:

* deployable field infrastructure
* durable industrial machine
* medium importance interactable
* capable of producing compound tools / infrastructure packages

It should **not** read as:

* sacred shrine
* boss arena device
* generic 3D printer
* giant factory hall

---

## 11.2 Locked descriptive design

The Compound Fabricator should have:

### Required components

* central fabrication chamber or cradle
* visible tool head / armature / fabrication rig
* clear front interaction face
* output tray / front lip / front ramp
* one side utility cluster
* one power/storage mass
* one small display or control panel
* limited teal/cyan operational lighting
* Custodian insignia in one or two deliberate positions

### Optional components

* one signal mast
* one tank
* one vent stack
* one side crate cluster
* one brace or handrail system

### Forbidden components

* giant cathedral canopy
* 4+ ceremonial banners
* extreme symmetrical ornamentation
* altar basin dominance
* more than one heroic smokestack on a utility-class fabricator
* huge holograms
* massive exposed hex glyph centerpiece
* wide open platform with no protected housing

---

## 11.3 Best current direction

Of the recent explorations, the **third variation** is the best baseline.

Why:

* best readable front
* best deployment / utility feel
* clear chamber + console + ramp logic
* strongest "compound infrastructure" read
* most aligned with the game's world scale

Refine it by:

* simplifying minor side clutter
* reducing decorative extras by ~15–25%
* keeping chamber + ramp + control face as the focal trio
* preserving the compact heavy footprint

# 13. Artist / Generator Prompt Contract

Any future prompt or brief for Custodian structures should include these constraints:

## Required prompt language

* dark industrial field infrastructure
* top-down 2D game structure
* readable at gameplay scale
* grim fortified utility
* weathered steel and stone-metal composite
* restrained teal/cyan operational lighting
* subtle Custodian insignia
* clear front interaction face
* no shrine dominance
* no glossy generic sci-fi
* transparent background

## Required anti-drift language

* avoid cathedral / altar vibes
* avoid excessive ornament
* avoid cluttered over-detailing
* avoid consumer sci-fi polish
* avoid giant decorative glow
* avoid unclear silhouette

---

# 14. Final Locked Summary

If someone asks, "What is a Custodian structure?" the answer should be:

> A Custodian structure is a fortified industrial machine or installation built by an organized ruined authority. It is weathered, functional, and readable first, with restrained holy-tech undertones, a dark steel-and-stone material language, limited teal operational light, and a clear gameplay-facing interaction side.

And if they ask, "What is a Custodian fabricator?":

> A Custodian fabricator is a compact field-deployable industrial production node: a squat armored machine with a central fabrication chamber, a readable front access face, a small control interface, one or two support modules, restrained signal lighting, and minimal but deliberate faction identity.
